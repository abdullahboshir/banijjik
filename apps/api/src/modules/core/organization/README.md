# core/organization: organizations, locations and members

A signed-in user creates a business and lists every business they belong to.

- **Entities:** `Organization`, `Location`, `Member` (platform database, 09 sections 4.2 to 4.4). `Role` belongs to `core/iam`.
- **Public API** (`index.ts`): `registerOrganizationModels`, `buildOrganizationRouters`, `organizationManifest`.
- **Endpoints** (`/api/v1`): `POST /me/orgs`, `GET /me/orgs`; since card 1.7b under `/orgs/{organizationId}`: `GET` and `PATCH /profile`, `/locations` (list, create, `PATCH`, `archive`), `/members` (list, detail, `PATCH`, `suspend`, `reactivate`, `remove`), `/invitations` (list, create, `revoke`); user level: `GET /invitations/{token}` (public) and `POST /invitations/{token}/accept`.
- **Staff rules (ADR-092):** the OWNER role is never assignable, nobody changes their own access, the owner member is protected, roles must be ACTIVE, and the caller must already hold every permission key of every role they give or take away (no privilege escalation). Suspended and removed members lose access on the next request. See `domain/assignment-rules.ts` and `application/assignment-guard.ts`.
- **Invitations:** the token is random, only its SHA-256 is stored, it is returned once (`inviteUrlPath`) and masked in logs; it expires after 7 days; the invitee's email or phone must equal the invited one. Delivery by SMS or email comes with card 5.13.
- **Limits:** `locations` (ACTIVE locations) and `members` (ACTIVE members plus unexpired pending invitations) through the kernel `LimitGuard`.
- **Creation:** one transaction writes the organization, its six system roles (through iam's `index.ts`), its default location and the owner member (ACTIVE, OWNER role at ALL). The MEMBER number is taken just before it (gaps allowed).
- **Defaults:** country BD, currency BDT, timezone Asia/Dhaka, locale = the creator's, deployment SHARED. None of them is read from the request.
- **Slug:** 3 to 40 characters of `a-z`, `0-9` and single hyphens, trimmed and lower-cased, not on the reserved list. A taken slug is `409 DUPLICATE` on `details.field = "slug"`.
- **Rate limit:** 5 creations per user per 24 hours (interim until a verified identifier is required).
- **Listing:** three queries however many organizations; CLOSED ones are left out, SUSPENDED ones show their status.
- **Events:** emits `organization.organization.created` and `.renamed`, `organization.location.created`, `.updated`, `.archived`, `organization.member.invited`, `.joined`, `.assignmentsChanged`, `.suspended`, `.reactivated`, `.removed`, `organization.invitation.revoked` (ids only, written with the change in one transaction).
- **Permissions:** declares `organization.profile.update`, `organization.location.{read,create,update,archive}`, `organization.member.{read,invite,update,remove,readSensitive}`, `organization.ownership.transfer` (the two sensitive ones marked). The card 1.7b endpoints use them; reading the profile needs no key.
- **For iam (card 1.4):** `Organization.rolesVersion` (bumped by `buildRolesVersionBumper()`), and `buildMemberAccessReader()` (a member's assignments and the one-aggregate role member counts). The membership directory also returns `Member.version` and `rolesVersion` for the permission cache key.
- **Tenancy (card 1.3):** `Organization` is `NONE`; `Location` and `Member` are `ORGANIZATION` (tenant plugin, ADR-087). The module implements the kernel port `MembershipDirectory` (`buildMembershipDirectory()`, exported for `routes/v1.ts`). Organization creation runs as the new organization through the injected `OrganizationContextRunner`; `GET /me/orgs` spans organizations, so `routes/v1.ts` wraps `buildMyOrganizationsReader()` as system access (AUTH_LOOKUP).
- **Suspend and reactivate (card 1.8a):** `SuspendOrganizationUseCase` and `ReactivateOrganizationUseCase` (`buildOrganizationStatusUseCases`) change the status with `version`, bump `configVersion`, publish `organization.organization.suspended|reactivated` and write a platform audit entry in one transaction; the platform API calls them. A suspended organization answers `403 ORG_SUSPENDED` from `tenantContext` on the next request. `buildOrganizationDirectory()` serves the platform tenant list and detail (read under `runAsSystem(PLATFORM_ADMIN)` by the caller).
- **Industry key:** stored as a plain field and never branched on (ADR-033, ADR-082).
- **Trial (card 1.6a):** creation also starts a 14-day BUNDLE trial subscription inside the same transaction, through the `TrialStarter` port (implemented by `platform/saas-billing`, wired in `routes/v1.ts`). Without a seeded BUNDLE package the business is not created. The module also exposes `buildConfigVersionBumper()` (bumps `Organization.configVersion`, keys the resolved-config cache) and `buildOrganizationConfigSource()` (template key and config version for the resolver); the membership directory returns `configVersion` fresh on every request.
- **Not here:** template publish check (2.1), slug change and history, ownership transfer, close of the organization, custom fields on members (2.3), SMS or email delivery of invitations (5.13), the admin menu entries (see the 1.7b handoff).
