# Plan: card 3.11 · Staff commission

Tier: **T1** (money owed to staff, ledgers, accounting) · Planned by: Opus 5.5 (Claude) · Date: 2026-10-23 · Based on commit: `main 7ddfb1f`

Phase 3, wave 5. Inputs merged: 3.6 (bookings with `providerMemberId`), 3.8 Part A (payments, `PaymentSummary.refunded`), 3.8c (accounting engine). Reserved ADR number: **ADR-135** (refines ADR-100; written in `04-DECISIONS.md` with this plan).

Owner decisions (2026-10-23, all four on the planner's recommendation):
- **Base** = the price after discount, excluding tax.
- **Refund after completion** reduces the commission in proportion. If the commission was already paid out, the next payout nets it off.
- **Expense timing:** commission expense and the payable to staff are posted when the commission accrues (service completed); a payout settles the payable. This changes ADR-100, which said "payout posts the expense".
- **Two role rules for one member** that match equally: the one that gives the **higher** commission wins. A rule on the member always beats rules on their roles.

## Goal
A service business (salon, gym, coaching centre) sets commission rules: per member or role, and per offering, category, kind or everything. Each rule is a percentage or a fixed amount. When a booking with a provider is completed, the provider's commission is accrued as an append-only ledger entry and posted as expense against a payable to staff. A refund after completion lowers it in proportion. A manager pays a member's open commission at a location as one payout, which settles the payable in the books. Staff see their own commission.

**Module** (`commerce/commission`, OPTIONAL, `MODULE_KEY.COMMISSION = "commission"`)

   - Package: a new add-on `PACKAGE_KEY.ADDON_COMMISSION` with `[MODULE_KEY.COMMISSION]` (`seed-packages.ts`, add-only). It is not in any base package; pricing is data and the owner can change it later.
   - Permissions:
     - `commission.rule.read`, `commission.rule.update`
     - `commission.entry.read`: all members at the caller's locations
     - `commission.entry.readOwn`: the caller's own entries
     - `commission.payout.read`, `commission.payout.create`
   - System role row (add-only in `SYSTEM_ROLE_GRANTS`):
     - `OWNER: "ALL"`
     - `MANAGER: "ALL"`
     - `STAFF: { only: [COMMISSION_ENTRY_READ_OWN] }`
     - the other system roles: `{ only: [COMMISSION_ENTRY_READ_OWN] }` if they are staff-like (rider, cashier), otherwise `"NONE"`. The implementer lists the six roles in the handoff with the value chosen; when unsure, `"NONE"`.
   - When the module is off: the routes return the standard module-off response, and the handlers do nothing. For handlers, set `moduleKey: MODULE_KEY.COMMISSION`: `kernel/events/handler-runner.ts` already skips a handler whose module is not effective for the organization.

2. **CommissionRule** (organization scope, versioned, archived never deleted):
   - Fields: `commissionRuleId`, `subjectType` (`MEMBER | ROLE`), `memberId?`, `roleId?` (exactly one, matching `subjectType`), `targetType` (`ALL | KIND | CATEGORY | OFFERING`), `offeringKind?`, `categoryId?`, `offeringId?` (exactly the one matching `targetType`; none for `ALL`), `calculation` (`PERCENT | FIXED`), `percentBasisPoints?` (integer 1–10 000, so 12.5 % = 1250; PERCENT only), `fixedAmount?: Money` (amount > 0, FIXED only), `status` (`ACTIVE | ARCHIVED`), audit fields, `version`.
   - Validation on create and update:
     - the member, role, category and offering exist in this organization (404 otherwise)
     - `offeringKind` is one of the organization's enabled kinds, from `resolveOrgConfig`
     - `fixedAmount.currency` equals the organization currency (`422 errors.commission_currency_mismatch`)
   - **One active rule per slot:** a unique partial index on `{ organizationId, subjectType, subjectId, targetType, targetValue }` where `status: ACTIVE`. `subjectId` and `targetValue` are stored denormalised strings; `targetValue` is `"*"` for ALL. A duplicate is `409 errors.commission_rule_duplicate` with the existing rule's id.
   - Endpoints:
     - `GET /commission/rules?subjectType&memberId&roleId&status&page&limit` (`commission.rule.read`)
     - `POST /commission/rules`
     - `PATCH /commission/rules/:commissionRuleId` (with `version`; 409 when stale)
     - `POST /commission/rules/:commissionRuleId/archive` (with `version`)
     - Writes need `commission.rule.update`, write one audit entry each, and need no idempotency key (they create no money).
   - Editing a rule never changes entries already written. Each entry keeps a snapshot of the rule it used.

3. **Rule resolution** (pure domain function `resolveCommissionRule`, fully unit-tested):
   - Inputs: the provider `memberId`; the member's role ids at the booking's location (assignments at that `locationId` or at `ALL`); the offering's `offeringId`, `kind` and `categoryIds`; the base; the quantity; the active rules of the organization for that member and those roles.
   - Order: first the MEMBER rules, then the ROLE rules. Within one subject level, try the targets from most specific to least: `OFFERING`, then `CATEGORY` (the offering is in that category), then `KIND`, then `ALL`.
   - The first (level, target) step with at least one matching rule decides. If that step has several rules (two categories, or two roles), the rule with the **higher computed amount** wins; on a tie, the older `createdAt`, then the lower `commissionRuleId`.
   - No match: no entry. Log at debug level `commission.no_rule` with the booking id; it is not an error.

4. **Amount** (pure domain function `computeCommissionAmount`):
   - `base` = booking `price.total − price.tax` (after discount, without tax; owner decision).
   - PERCENT: `floor(base × percentBasisPoints / 10 000)`, in integer minor units.
   - FIXED: `fixedAmount × quantity`.
   - The result is clamped to `0 … base`: commission never exceeds the base. A base of 0 gives no entry.
   - **Currency:** the rule currency and the booking currency are compared, never assumed (§11 item 3). On a mismatch: no entry, a warning log `commission.accrual_skipped_currency` with the booking id and rule id, and a counter in the handler result.

5. **CommissionEntry** (location scope, append-only ledger; ADR-014):
   - Fields:
     - `commissionEntryId`, `memberId`, `locationId`
     - `sourceType` (`BOOKING`; `ORDER_LINE` arrives with Part C), `sourceId`, `sourceLineId: null`
     - `sequence` (integer, 1 for the accrual, then 2, 3, … for adjustments of the same source and member)
     - `entryKind` (`ACCRUAL | REFUND_ADJUSTMENT`)
     - `amount: Money` (signed: an accrual is > 0; an adjustment is < 0 when a refund lowers it and > 0 when a refund reversal restores it)
     - `base: Money`, `sourceGrossTotal: Money` (`price.total`, including tax; the refund ratio uses it)
     - `refundedTotalSeen: Money` (the source's refunded total this entry brought the accrual in line with; 0 for an accrual)
     - `ruleSnapshot: { commissionRuleId, subjectType, targetType, calculation, percentBasisPoints?, fixedAmount? }`
     - `businessDate` (organization local date)
     - `payoutId: null | PayoutId` (set once by A2)
     - audit fields; **no `version`**: the record is append-only, and only `payoutId` changes, once, by a conditional update
   - Indexes:
     - unique `{ organizationId, sourceType, sourceId, sourceLineId, memberId, sequence }`: stops a double accrual and a double adjustment (§11 item 2)
     - `{ organizationId, memberId, businessDate: -1, commissionEntryId: -1 }` (lists)
     - `{ organizationId, locationId, memberId, payoutId, businessDate }` (open entries for a payout)
   - **Business date:** the organization's local date of the event's `occurredAt`, through a `TimezoneReader` port adapted like `routes/booking-deposit-adapter.ts`. Never `toISOString().slice(0, 10)` (§11 item 13).

6. **Accrual and refund adjustment** (handler `commission.accrue`, `ordering: "ORDERED"`):
   - The handler handles `booking.booking.completed`, and also `payment.payment.refunded` and `payment.payment.reversed` (`alsoHandles`). Booking and payment events run on different ordering keys (`booking:<id>` and payment's own key), so the design must not depend on their order.
   - **On `booking.booking.completed`:**
     - read the booking through a `CommissionSourceReader` port: `bookingId`, `locationId`, `providerMemberId`, `offeringId`, `quantity`, `price`, `status`
     - no provider, or a status other than COMPLETED: stop
     - otherwise read the rules, offering and assignments, resolve, compute, and insert the ACCRUAL (`sequence: 1`) and emit `commission.entry.recorded`, in one `withTransaction()`
     - then run **reconcile** (below) in the same transaction
     - a duplicate-key error on `sequence: 1` means a replay: return without error
   - **On `payment.payment.refunded` / `payment.payment.reversed`:** read the payment's allocations through a `CommissionPaymentReader` port. For each allocation with `requestType: BOOKING`, run reconcile for that booking. An allocation with no accrual is skipped: if the booking is completed later, the accrual runs reconcile itself.
   - **Reconcile(source, member)** (pure computation plus one insert):
     - `refunded` = the source's current `PaymentSummary.refunded`, through the payment port, capped at `sourceGrossTotal`
     - `targetNet` = `accrual − floor(accrual × refunded / sourceGrossTotal)`
     - `currentNet` = Σ `amount` of all entries of the source and member
     - `delta` = `targetNet − currentNet`. If `delta` is 0, stop. Otherwise insert a REFUND_ADJUSTMENT with `amount = delta`, `sequence = max + 1`, `refundedTotalSeen = refunded`, and emit `commission.entry.recorded`.
     - Two handlers that race insert the same `sequence`: the loser gets a duplicate key and **retries once** with a fresh read (inside `runWithTxnRetry`-style logic; reuse the booking module's helper only through an index, or write a local one). A replay finds `delta = 0`.
   - Refunds recorded **before** completion also lower the commission: the accrual reconciles at once. This is consistent with the owner's "after discount" rule, because money returned before the service is in effect a discount.
   - The negative running total of a member (refunds after payout) stays as open negative entries; A2 nets them in the next payout.
   - **Events** (contracts `COMMISSION_EVENT_TYPE`): `commission.entry.recorded` `{ commissionEntryId }`, ordering key `commission:<memberId>`. Audit action `commission.rule.created | updated | archived` (contracts).

**Part A2**

7. **CommissionPayout** (location scope, number from a new sequence `COMMISSION_PAYOUT`, append-only):
   - Fields: `commissionPayoutId`, `payoutNumber`, `memberId`, `locationId`, `upToBusinessDate`, `entryCount`, `total: Money` (> 0), `method` (`CASH | BKASH | NAGAD | BANK`), `externalReference?` (required for BKASH and NAGAD), `note?`, `businessDate`, audit fields.
   - Endpoints:
     - `GET /commission/payouts/preview?memberId&location&upTo` (`commission.payout.create`) returns `{ entryCount, total }` of the open entries: `payoutId: null`, that member and location, `businessDate ≤ upTo`
     - `POST /commission/payouts` (`commission.payout.create`, **requires `Idempotency-Key`**)
     - `GET /commission/payouts?memberId&location&page&limit`, `GET /commission/payouts/:commissionPayoutId` (`commission.payout.read`)
   - Create body: `{ memberId, locationId, upToBusinessDate, expectedTotal: Money, method, externalReference?, note? }`. `locationId` is validated against the caller's assignments. In one `withTransaction()`:
     1. Claim with `updateMany({ organizationId, memberId, locationId, payoutId: null, businessDate ≤ upTo }, { $set: { payoutId } })`. Only entries that were open are claimed, so two payouts can never take the same entry.
     2. Sum the claimed entries with an aggregate on `payoutId` (one query, no array of all rows in memory).
     3. If the sum ≤ 0: abort, `422 errors.commission_payout_nothing_to_pay`.
     4. If the sum ≠ `expectedTotal`: abort, `409 errors.commission_payout_total_changed` with the new total (an entry arrived or a refund changed it after the preview).
     5. Insert the payout, emit `commission.payout.paid` `{ commissionPayoutId }`, write an audit entry.
   - **No void in this card.** A wrong payout is corrected with a manual journal entry (3.8b); this is a known gap in STATUS.

8. **Accounting** (A2; ADR-135):
   - Two new system accounts, add-only in contracts and seeds: `COMMISSION_EXPENSE` (EXPENSE) and `COMMISSION_PAYABLE` (LIABILITY). Organizations created earlier get them through the existing `catchUpSystemAccounts`; a test proves it.
   - `JOURNAL_SOURCE_TYPE.COMMISSION`, and posting keys `commission_accrued`, `commission_adjusted`, `commission_paid` (add-only).
   - Handler `accounting.postCommission` (in `commerce/accounting`, ORDERED, registered only when a `CommissionPostingSource` adapter is supplied, like expenses), on `commission.entry.recorded` and `commission.payout.paid`:
     - **Accrual:** Dr `COMMISSION_EXPENSE` / Cr `COMMISSION_PAYABLE`
     - **Adjustment < 0:** Dr `COMMISSION_PAYABLE` / Cr `COMMISSION_EXPENSE`, for the absolute amount
     - **Adjustment > 0:** the same lines as an accrual
     - **Payout:** Dr `COMMISSION_PAYABLE` / Cr the method account (`METHOD_ACCOUNT_CODE`)
     - `sourceId` = the entry id or the payout id. The journal's unique `(sourceType, sourceId, postingKey)` makes a replay a no-op.
   - The commission module supplies `buildCommissionPostingSourceAdapter()` from its `index.ts`, and `routes/accounting-event-handlers.ts` passes it (add-only).

## Part C outline (refreshed by the lead after 3.5a Part A merges)
- `sourceType: ORDER_LINE`, `sourceId = orderId`, `sourceLineId = orderLineId`. Base = the line's net total without tax.
- Accrual on the order's completion event. Reconcile uses the order's `PaymentSummary` scaled to the line's share, or `sales.return.created` per line (decide at refresh).
- No change to A1's rules or resolution.

## Out of scope
- Payout void or edit, salary, attendance, targets or tiered (slab) commission, commission on enrollments or plans.
- Commission on order lines before Part C.
- Changing booking or payment behaviour. The booking posting source's UTC business date is a separate follow-up (see Risks).
- Any setting. Rules are the only configuration.

## Risks & checks
- **Event order:** booking and payment events are on different streams. Reconcile from the summary makes order irrelevant; the "refund before completion" test proves it.
- **Double payout:** the claim is a conditional `updateMany` on `payoutId: null` inside the transaction, and the total check aborts on drift.
- **Rounding:** the target is computed from the cumulative refund each time, never by adding per-refund roundings, so a full refund leaves exactly 0.
- **Found while planning (not this card):** `commerce/booking/application/booking-posting-source.ts` posts bookings with `startsAt.toISOString().slice(0, 10)` (a UTC date, §11 item 13) and `kind: "SERVICE"` for every booking. The lead tracks this as a follow-up; 3.6b A1 may fix `kind`.
- Run `npm run card:check`; check the new route sizes in the CI build log (Part B).
