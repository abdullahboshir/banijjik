## Goal
Wire the `report.summarize` event handler to count immutable delivery & loss facts into daily report summaries:
1. `sales.order.shipped`:
   - When an order ships with `COURIER` or `OWN_RIDER`, consume immutable `firstShipment` facts (`netRevenueMinor`, `taxTotalMinor`, `deliveryChargeMinor`, `grandTotalMinor`, `discountTotalMinor`, `subtotalMinor`).
   - Increments `ordersCompleted`, `grossSales`, `discountTotal`, `taxTotal`, `deliveryCharges`, and `netSales`.
   - Subsequent reshipment attempts (attempt 2+) do not duplicate report summaries (idempotent / only on first shipment).
2. `sales.order.returned`:
   - Increments `returnsCount` by 1 and `returnsValue` by the return value.
3. `sales.order.lossClosed`:
   - Increments `returnsCount` by 1 and `returnsValue` by the returned/restocked value.
