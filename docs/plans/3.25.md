# Plan: card 3.25 · Local business dates and held advances in booking posting

Tier: **T1** (small, but it changes journal dates and amounts) · Planned by: Opus 5.5 (Claude) · Date: 2026-10-23 · Based on commit: `main c11f0c7`

## Session parts
One part, API only. Branch `s/3.25-posting-dates-advances`. T1 review by the lead (short).

## Goal
Three wrong numbers reach the ledger today:
1. `booking/application/booking-posting-source.ts` uses `startsAt.toISOString().slice(0, 10)`: a booking at 01:00 Dhaka time is posted on the previous day (§11 item 13).
2. The same file sets `advancesToSettle: booking.depositRequired`: a booking whose deposit was required but not paid moves money out of `CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` that was never there, and a customer who paid more than the deposit before the visit leaves the extra stuck in advances.
3. `credit/application/use-cases/business-date.ts` returns the UTC date for credit entries and purchases (matrimony track).

After this card, every booking and credit posting uses the organization's local date, and a completed booking settles exactly the advance the customer paid before revenue.

## Inputs read
- `booking/application/booking-posting-source.ts`, `booking/domain/timezone.ts` (`businessDateOf(instant, timeZone)`), `booking-org-adapter.ts` (time zone reader), `booking/index.ts` (`buildBookingPostingSourceAdapter`)
- accounting `domain/posting-rules.ts` (`BookingPostingSnapshot`, `draftBookingCompleted`, how refunds of BEFORE_REVENUE allocations are posted)
- payment `infrastructure/payment.schema.ts` (allocations carry `requestType`, `requestId`, `revenueState`; directions IN, REFUND, REVERSAL), `application/ports/payment-repositories.ts`, `index.ts`
- `credit/application/use-cases/business-date.ts` and its callers

## Decisions
1. **Booking business date:** the posting source reads the organization time zone (the existing booking time zone reader, required, no fallback literal) and uses `businessDateOf(booking.endsAt, timeZone)` for the completed entry: revenue is earned when the service ends. Cancellation-fee and no-show entries use the date of the status change: the last history entry's time (`history[].at` or the field the entity uses) in the organization time zone. If the history has no time, stop and ask.
2. **Held advance reader** (payment module, new port + Mongo adapter, exported from `payment/index.ts` as a function, not a class):
   ```ts
   export interface RequestAdvanceReader {
     /** Net BEFORE_REVENUE money the request holds: IN allocations minus the refunds and reversals of them. */
     readHeldAdvance(input: { organizationId: OrganizationId; requestType: PaymentRequestType; requestId: string }): Promise<Money>;
   }
   ```
   - It follows the exact rule the payment posting uses to decide whether a refund or reversal debits `CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` (read `posting-rules.ts`; do not invent a second rule). Never below zero.
   - New multikey index on payments `{ organizationId: 1, "allocations.requestType": 1, "allocations.requestId": 1 }`; the read uses it with a projection.
   - Card 3.24 registers ORDER with it; this card covers BOOKING (and ENROLLMENT if the same query works unchanged; say which in the handoff).
3. **Booking posting snapshot:** `advancesToSettle` = `readHeldAdvance(BOOKING, bookingId)` at posting time. A payment recorded after completion is `AFTER_REVENUE` and is not counted (that is already how payment captures the state).
4. **Credit business date:** `credit/.../business-date.ts` takes the organization time zone (from the organization module's reader, through the credit module's existing ports) and returns the local date; every caller passes it. No UTC fallback.
5. **Records:** a note under ADR-124 in `docs/04-DECISIONS.md` ("posting dates are local; advances settle from the held amount"), booking, payment and credit READMEs, handoff `docs/handoffs/3.25-api.md` with the §11 table, STATUS row 3.25. Remove the STATUS/LEAD follow-up line this card closes.
