## Session parts
One part, API only. Branch `s/3.31-api-enrollment-revenue`. T1 review by the lead. No screen work: journals show in the existing accounting screens.

## Goal
Today every enrollment payment stays in `CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` forever, because no enrollment income is ever posted (ADR-139). After this card:
- each period's fee is posted as plan revenue once, when the period is in service;
- the advances paid for it move to the receivable;
- payments after that point are `AFTER_REVENUE`;
- a cancelled enrollment's periods end with revenue equal to what the business keeps.

The books then match the dues the payment module shows.

## Decisions
1. **When a period earns its revenue.** A period's whole fee is recognised once, when the period is in service. In service means:
   - its start date (organization local date) has come;
   - its enrollment is `ACTIVE`, `OVERDUE` or `FROZEN`.

   `PENDING` is not in service: the customer has not started.

   The fee is recognised whole at the start, not spread day by day. This is the common practice for short club and course periods; spreading the fee is out of scope.

   A `WAIVED` period and a zero fee post nothing.
2. **Where it is recorded: the period.** New optional field `revenueRecognizedOn?: string` (local date), set once and never changed. Setting it is the only way revenue starts.

   One helper `recognizePeriodRevenue(dependencies, enrollment, period, businessDate)` writes it. In the same transaction it:
   - saves the period with its `version` (stale → 409);
   - touches the payment summary through the port (decision 4);
   - publishes the event (decision 3).

   Calling the helper on a period that already has `revenueRecognizedOn` does nothing.

   It is called inside the existing transactions only, with these dates:

   | Call site | Period | `businessDate` |
   |---|---|---|
   | **activate** (manual, and the job's payment-driven activation) | the current period, which has started (START_DATE rule) | the local date of activation |
   | **renew** (job and manual) | the new current period, once the enrollment is not `PENDING` and the period has started | its `periodStartsOn`, because service ran without a break |
   | **cancel** | decision 6 | the cancel date |

   No other path starts a period, so no extra job is needed. The renew job already catches up "on or after", hourly (§11 item 10).

   An advance period paid early and started later is recognised by the renew transition that makes it current.
3. **Event.** `ENROLLMENT_EVENT_TYPE.PERIOD_REVENUE_RECOGNIZED = "enrollment.period.revenueRecognized"`, payload `{ enrollmentId, enrollmentPeriodId }`.
   - It carries `orderingKey: enrollmentPeriod:<enrollmentPeriodId>`.
   - It is published in the helper's transaction.
   - It is added to contracts and the event payload map (add-only).
4. **Race with a payment (ADR-138 R3).** The helper touches the period's payment summary in its transaction, the way order completion does: `EnrollmentPaymentPort.touchForRevenue({ organizationId, enrollmentPeriodId, total: period.fee, now })`. It calls `initializeSummary` if the summary is missing, then sets the same total.

   A payment that read the period as `BEFORE_REVENUE` and commits later conflicts and retries. After the retry the reader returns `AFTER_REVENUE`, so the payment goes to the receivable, not to advances.
5. **Reader revenue state.** `routes/enrollment-request-reader.ts` returns `AFTER_REVENUE` when `revenueRecognizedOn` is set, else `BEFORE_REVENUE`. Update the comment and ADR-139's sentence about 3.31.
6. **Cancel closes every period at what the business keeps.** The cancel transaction already caps each non-waived summary at its net paid. After capping, for each non-waived period with net paid N and fee F:
   - **Recognised period:** publish `ENROLLMENT_EVENT_TYPE.PERIOD_REVENUE_CLOSED` with `{ enrollmentId, enrollmentPeriodId, keptAmount: N, recognizedAmount: F }` (Money). The handler posts `enrollment_period_revenue_cancel`: Dr `REVENUE_PLAN` / Cr `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` for F − N, only when that is more than 0.
   - **Not recognised, N > 0:** set `revenueRecognizedOn` = cancel date through the helper, in the same transaction, and publish `PERIOD_REVENUE_CLOSED` with `recognizedAmount: N`. The handler then posts:
     - `enrollment_period_cancellation_fee`: Dr `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` / Cr `CANCELLATION_FEE_INCOME` for N. Money kept for a period that never started is a cancellation fee, as for bookings.
     - `enrollment_period_advance_settle` as in decision 7.

     The revenue event of decision 3 is not published for these periods: `PERIOD_REVENUE_CLOSED` replaces it.
   - **Not recognised, N = 0:** nothing.

   A refund paid later (card 3.32) lowers the kept amount and posts its own entries. This card only states that `keptAmount` is the net paid at cancel.
7. **Postings** (accounting, new handler `accounting.postEnrollments`, ORDERED, `streamPrefixes: ["enrollmentPeriod:"]`, idempotent on the journal source key):
   - **Revenue** on `PERIOD_REVENUE_RECOGNIZED`:
     - `enrollment_period_revenue`: Dr `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` F / Cr `REVENUE_PLAN` F. `sourceType ENROLLMENT` (new `JOURNAL_SOURCE_TYPE`, add-only), `sourceId` = `enrollmentPeriodId`, `businessDate` = `revenueRecognizedOn`, `locationId` = the period's.
     - then `enrollment_period_advance_settle`: Dr `CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` / Cr `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` for the held advance from `RequestAdvanceReader` (`PAYMENT_REQUEST_TYPE.ENROLLMENT`, the period id), only when it is more than 0. Never the fee and never the net paid: R1 of 3.24.
   - **Close** on `PERIOD_REVENUE_CLOSED`: the entries of decision 6, then the advance settle as above. The settle key is shared, so a period settles once.
   - The snapshot is read through an enrollment posting-source port (`buildEnrollmentPostingSourceAdapter`, the way `buildOrderPostingSourceAdapter` works), with required dependencies.
   - A missing period or a currency mismatch throws `postingSourceMissing`. The outbox keeps the event; nothing is posted to a guessed account.
8. **Posting keys and accounts.** New `POSTING_KEY` values (add-only):
   - `enrollment_period_revenue`
   - `enrollment_period_advance_settle`
   - `enrollment_period_revenue_cancel`
   - `enrollment_period_cancellation_fee`

   No new account. `REVENUE_PLAN`, `CANCELLATION_FEE_INCOME`, `CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` and `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` exist, and older organizations get them through `ensureSystemAccount` if missing.
9. **3.34 write-off rule for ENROLLMENT.** 3.34 posts no bad-debt journal for an enrollment, because no income was recognised at cancel. After this card the kept amount is income. So a `WRITE_OFF` of a reversed payment on a cancelled enrollment period posts the same bad-debt entry 3.34 uses for a booking's retained fee, for the written-off amount, on the period's source. Change only the ENROLLMENT branch 3.34 adds, and take the account and posting key it uses. If 3.34 merged with a different shape, stop and ask.
10. **No backfill.** Periods that were in service before this card have no `revenueRecognizedOn`; the project has no production data. State it in the handoff.

## Out of scope
- Spreading revenue over the days of a period (deferred revenue schedules).
- Refund execution on cancel (3.32).
- Security deposits (not charged in the period fee today).
- Tax on plan fees (enrollment fees carry no tax today).
- Screens.

## Risks & checks
- Decision 4 is the T1 heart: a payment and a recognition must never both count one amount. A6 must race real writes.
- Decision 6 runs inside cancel, which already caps summaries: read N after the cap, in the same transaction.
- One period may get both `PERIOD_REVENUE_RECOGNIZED` (at its start) and later `PERIOD_REVENUE_CLOSED` (at cancel). They share the period's stream, so they post in order.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-25, lead, after the implementer's questions)
1. **The close event says how the period was recognised.** `PERIOD_REVENUE_CLOSED` carries `recognizedBeforeCancel: boolean` in addition to `keptAmount` and `recognizedAmount` (add-only in contracts and the payload map). `true`: the period had `revenueRecognizedOn` before the cancel transaction started (decision 6, first bullet) and the handler posts `enrollment_period_revenue_cancel` for F − N. `false`: the cancel path recognised it now (second bullet) and the handler posts `enrollment_period_cancellation_fee` for N. The handler branches on this flag only, never on comparing the amounts, because N can equal F.
2. **`touchForRevenue` never undoes the cancel cap.** It takes the total to write. Activate and renew pass the fee F. The cancel path passes the capped total the summary holds after `capTotalAtNetPaid` (N), so the touch still conflicts with a racing payment (decision 4) and dues keep showing what the business kept (A7). It must not clear `closedAt` (3.34).
3. **Periods in service before this card** have no `revenueRecognizedOn`; if such a period is cancelled with N > 0 it takes the cancellation-fee path. Accepted (no production data); write it in the handoff.

Tests to add: `the close event tells a period recognised at its start from one recognised at cancel even when the kept amount equals the fee` (N = F in both cases, different journals) and `cancel keeps the dues total at the kept amount after the revenue touch`.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-25, lead, after the implementer's question)
**Create as ACTIVE is a fourth call site.** `create-enrollment.ts` inserts an enrollment directly as `ACTIVE` when payment is not required and `startsOn` is today or earlier. That path starts the first period too, so decision 2's table gains a row: **create (status ACTIVE)** → the first period, `businessDate` = the creation date (`today`, organization local date). Call `recognizePeriodRevenue` in the create transaction **after** `insert` and `initializeSummary` (the touch needs the summary), with the touch total = the fee, and publish its event after `ENROLLMENT_CREATED`. A `PENDING` create recognises nothing. Test: `creating an enrollment that starts active recognises its first period on the creation date`. Existing tests that create an active enrollment and then cancel it now see a recognised period: if one of their expectations changes, stop and report `STATUS: QUESTION` with the test name and the old and new value; do not change it yourself.
