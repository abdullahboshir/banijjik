## Goal
Wire the accounting event stream handler `accounting.postOrders` to handle COD delivery transitions:
1. `sales.order.shipped`: Posts order revenue, tax, delivery income, advance settlement, and COGS on first shipment (idempotent; reshipment attempt 2+ does not duplicate).
2. `sales.order.returned`: Reverses first shipment journals (revenue, advances, COGS) on Return-To-Sender (RTS).
3. `sales.order.lossClosed`: Reverses recognized customer revenue and advances, reverses COGS for received units into inventory/damaged inventory, and reclassifies lost COGS into `DAMAGE_LOSS`.
4. `sales.deliveryLoss.recovered`: Debits inventory (available or damaged) and credits `DAMAGE_LOSS` for recovered amounts.

## Decisions
1. **Event stream ordering and fan-in:** All order transitions share the `order:<orderId>` stream prefix (`orderOrderingKey(orderId)`). `postOrders` handles `sales.order.completed`, `sales.order.shipped`, `sales.order.returned`, `sales.order.lossClosed`, and `sales.deliveryLoss.recovered`.
2. **Posting Keys and Idempotency:**
   - Shipped / Completed: `order_revenue`, `order_advance_settle`, `order_cost`. Natural idempotency on `(ORDER, orderId, postingKey)` guarantees that whether an order completes directly (POS / Pickup) or ships first, postings are made once and subsequent events replay cleanly.
   - RTS: `reversal_order_revenue`, `reversal_order_advance_settle`, `reversal_order_cost` (mirrored reversals via `postReversal`).
   - Delivery Loss Closed: `reversal_order_revenue`, `reversal_order_advance_settle`, `reversal_order_cost` (for received restocked/damaged units), and `delivery_loss` (reclassifying lost COGS to `DAMAGE_LOSS`).
   - Loss Recovery: `delivery_loss_recovery` (Debit INVENTORY/INVENTORY_DAMAGED, Credit DAMAGE_LOSS).
3. **Loss Account Code:** In line with `docs/plans/4.3-COD.md` Decision 4 & Audit correction ("Reuse the existing loss expense account if equivalent; seed a new system account only if none fits"), lost COGS is reclassified to `SYSTEM_ACCOUNT_CODE.DAMAGE_LOSS` (seeded expense account).
4. **Clean Architecture:** `post-orders.handler.ts` depends only on `OrderPostingSource` port. `OrderPostingSource` implementation in `commerce/sales` reads the order, first-shipment facts, and delivery loss repository.
