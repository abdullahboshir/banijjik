# Plan: card 3.40 · Walk-in services as order lines

Tier: Part A T1 (money, posting, commission) · Part B T2 (screens) · Planned by: Opus 5.5 (lead) · Date: 2026-10-26 · Based on commit: `main 619f78e0`

Phase 3 review F1 (`docs/handoffs/phase-3-review.md`). Design: `11 §5.3` ("A SERVICE sold on the spot without a slot is an Order line, with `performedByMemberId`. No booking."), ADR-044, ADR-065. Plan 3.5a moved walk-in service lines to 3.9; 3.9 did not take them.

## Owner decision (2026-10-26)
A walk-in service does **not** hold the performer's calendar or any resource. It is a sale, not a booking (the lead's recommendation, accepted: "তোমার পরামর্শ অনুযায়ী").

## Goal
A salon or gym sells a haircut or a training session on the spot, at the till or as an admin instant sale, with or without products in the same sale, names who performed it, and the books, the commission and the reports see it. Draft and placed orders (delivery orders) stay product-only.

## Decisions — Part A (API)
1. **Where a SERVICE line is allowed:** only in instant sales: `POST /orders/instant`, `POST /pos/sales`, and the new order of an exchange (all go through the instant prepare). `quoteAndPrepare` / `prepareOrderLines` take `allowedKinds: readonly OfferingKind[]`; the instant path passes `[PRODUCT, SERVICE]`, create, place and update pass `[PRODUCT]`. A kind outside the list stays `422 KIND_NEEDS_BOOKING` (PLAN, RENTAL and EVENT everywhere; SERVICE in draft and placed orders).
2. **Any ACTIVE SERVICE offering qualifies**, `bookingMode` `SLOT` or `WALK_IN_ONLY`. Nothing is written to bookings, slots, `resource_slot_usages` or time-off (owner decision). `requiresResource` is ignored for a walk-in sale.
3. **The kind must be effective:** a SERVICE line is refused with `422 KIND_NEEDS_BOOKING` when SERVICE is not among the organization's effective kinds (`resolveOrgConfig`, ADR-033). Add a small port `SalesKindReader { effectiveKinds(organizationId): Promise<readonly OfferingKind[]> }` adapted in `routes/` from the org config service; the instant path intersects `allowedKinds` with it. No `industry` read.
4. **No stock:** a SERVICE line never reaches the stock ledger, has `costComponents: []` and `unitCostSnapshot: null`, even if the offering document somehow carries stock components (defensive: take `[]` for SERVICE).
5. **Quantity:** an integer ≥ 1 for a SERVICE line (`validation.integer_required` at `lines.<i>.quantity` otherwise). Price, discounts, modifiers, tax, promotions and the price override come from the pricing quote exactly as for products.
6. **Performer:** `performedByMemberId` is optional, validated as today (ACTIVE, assigned at the sale's location or ALL, 3.11 C). Commission needs no change: `accrue-commission` matches rules by `offering.kind`.
7. **Books:** `OrderPostingSnapshot` gains `serviceGoods: Money` = Σ over SERVICE lines of `lineNetTotal + lineDiscount − lineTax` (the per-line form of the existing `goodsAmount`). `draftOrderCompleted` credits `REVENUE_SERVICE` with `serviceGoods` and `REVENUE_PRODUCT` with `goodsAmount − serviceGoods` (each only when > 0). The entry stays one balanced `order_revenue` journal. If `serviceGoods > goodsAmount` (should not happen), the posting throws `INTERNAL` rather than post a negative line.
8. **Returns and exchanges:** a SERVICE line is returnable like an untracked product line (no stock movement, no COGS reversal, `SALES_RETURNS` as today; commission reconciles by units, ADR-135 note). The return disposition is ignored for a SERVICE line; no new rule.
9. **Till catalog:** `GET /pos/catalog-snapshot` returns PRODUCT and SERVICE variants (kind is already in each row). The 500-row page limit and the keyset cursor stay.
10. **Reports:** nothing new. A walk-in sale is an order (`ORDER_COMPLETED`), counted in sales like any order.
11. **Contracts and docs:** no new error code, permission or event. Update `11 §5.3` with an implementation note (instant sales only; no calendar hold), the sales README, the `sales-line-preparation.ts` comment, and ADR-044's row with a one-line note ("walk-in services: card 3.40").

## Decisions — Part B (screens)
12. **Till (`apps/admin/src/pos`):** search and the local cache show services next to products (a small "service" badge); a service line has a whole-number stepper (no weight keypad) and a "Performed by" picker from `GET /sales/performers?locationId=<device location>` (already allowed for `sales.order.create`); the receipt prints the performer's display name under the line when set.
13. **Admin instant sale (`apps/admin/src/sales`):** when the form is in instant-sale mode the offering search includes services (`kind` PRODUCT and SERVICE: two parallel requests, merged by name, or one request per kind, whichever the existing `searchProducts` shape allows without an API change); draft and placed orders keep products only. The existing performer picker (3.11 C) works on service lines.
14. BN and EN keys for the badge and labels; mobile first; touch targets ≥ 44 px; route sizes within budget (`/pos` is at 166.8 of 180 KiB: keep new code in already lazy parts where possible).

## Tests (must exist and pass)
**Part A** (real Mongo through the API harness unless named as unit):
- `an instant sale sells a walk-in service with a product and completes in one request` (201; the order has two lines; one SALE movement for the product only; the service line has no movement)
- `a WALK_IN_ONLY service is sold at the till with its performer` (`POST /pos/sales`, `performedByMemberId` stored)
- `a draft or placed order still refuses a SERVICE line with KIND_NEEDS_BOOKING` (update the existing refusal test: SERVICE now allowed only in instant sales; PLAN still refused everywhere)
- `a SERVICE line is refused when SERVICE is not an effective kind` (module or kind switched off)
- `a walk-in service holds no slot` (after the sale, the performer's availability for that time is unchanged and no `resource_slot_usages` row exists)
- `a service quantity must be a whole number` (400 at `lines.0.quantity`)
- `the completed walk-in sale posts service revenue to REVENUE_SERVICE and product revenue to REVENUE_PRODUCT` (accounting handler through the worker path; the journal balances)
- unit `draftOrderCompleted splits goods between product and service revenue` (mixed; all-service has no `REVENUE_PRODUCT` line; all-product unchanged; order discount and tax shared)
- `a performed walk-in service accrues commission under a SERVICE rule` (3.11 rules; then a return of that line reconciles the accrual by units)
- `returning a service line writes no stock movement and refunds it` (3.5b path)
- `the till catalog snapshot includes active services and leaves out inactive ones`
- cross-org: `another organization's service cannot be sold` (404/400 as the catalog reader answers today, no order written); permission: `a member without sales.order.create cannot make an instant sale with a service` (403)

**Part B:**
- Unit: the till cart gives a service line a whole-number stepper and no weight keypad; the sale body carries `performedByMemberId` for a service line; the admin search includes services only in instant-sale mode.
- Browser (admin, POS): pair, open, add a service and a product, pick the performer, pay cash, the receipt shows both lines and the performer; route budget stays green.

## Out of scope
- Holding the calendar or a resource for a walk-in (owner: no).
- Service lines in draft, placed or delivery orders.
- PLAN, RENTAL or EVENT at the till (enrollments and bookings stay their own flows).
- Splitting `SALES_RETURNS` by kind.
