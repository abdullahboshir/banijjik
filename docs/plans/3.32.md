## Goal
Today cancelling an enrollment only tells staff a `refundableAmount` (policy `NONE`, `UNUSED_PERIODS` or `PRORATED`). The money cannot be returned: the enrollment request reader answers `undefined` for every period of a cancelled enrollment, so a refund is refused.

After this card:
- staff can refund up to the policy amount, per period, to store credit or by a payment method;
- refunds follow the existing refund approval rules;
- the refunded period does not come back as a due;
- the books lower the income that 3.31 kept at cancel.

## Decisions
1. **Refunds go through the existing refund endpoint.** No new refund route. Staff refund the periods of a cancelled enrollment with the normal payment refund (`POST /orgs/:organizationId/refunds`, allocations to the period ids). The method is a payment method or `STORE_CREDIT`. Approval works as for every other refund (the refund-without-approval permission, or an approval token). Refunding inside the cancel request is rejected: it would need the refund use case and its approval flow inside the cancel transaction.
2. **The amount each period may refund is fixed at cancel.** Add `refundableByPeriod(periods, today, policy)` next to `refundableAmount` in `domain/refunds.ts`, returning `Money` per period id; `refundableAmount` becomes the sum of it. Rules:
   - only PAID periods count;
   - `UNUSED_PERIODS`: the fee of each period that starts after today;
   - `PRORATED`: the unused days of the current period **and** the fee of each period that starts after today (a paid future period is wholly unused; today's code leaves it out — this refresh fixes that; owner told);
   - `NONE`: zero for every period.

   In the cancel transaction, before `capPeriodSummaries`, store on each period `refundableOnCancel: Money` (new optional field, set once). The cancel response keeps `refundableAmount` (the sum) and adds `refundableByPeriod: { enrollmentPeriodId, amount }[]`.
3. **How each period closed, stored at cancel.** `closeOnePeriod` (3.31) stores on the period `revenueClosedAs: "REVENUE" | "CANCELLATION_FEE"` (new contract enum `ENROLLMENT_REVENUE_CLOSE`), from its `recognizedBeforeCancel`, in the same transaction. A period that closes with nothing kept stores nothing. Dates are never compared to find this: a never-recognised period gets `revenueRecognizedOn` = the cancel date, so a date cannot tell the two cases apart.
4. **Readers state whether a request accepts payments and how much may be refunded.** `RequestReadSnapshot` gets two required fields:
   - `acceptsPayments: boolean`. Booking and order readers answer `true` wherever they answer today (set explicitly, no default).
   - `refundLimit: Money | null`: `null` means no extra limit.

   The enrollment reader:
   - a period of a **cancelled** enrollment: answer the period with `acceptsPayments: false` and `refundLimit` = `refundableOnCancel` (zero when unset) minus what was refunded on that period since the cancel (decision 6), never below zero; `revenueState: AFTER_REVENUE`;
   - every other period: `acceptsPayments: true`, `refundLimit: null`, as today;
   - `WAIVED` periods: still `undefined`.
5. **Payment enforces the two fields, request-neutrally.**
   - Receive refuses a request with `acceptsPayments: false` with the same error a missing request gets today.
   - The refund validation, inside the refund transaction, refuses an allocation above its `refundLimit` with `PAYMENT_REFUND_ABOVE_LIMIT` (new rule key in contracts, `BUSINESS_RULE` with `details: { ruleKey, requestId, limit }`).
   - The reader reads the summary and the period with the active session, so two parallel refunds cannot both pass (§11 item 2). The summary write of the first refund conflicts with the second transaction's read-then-write, and the retry sees the new `refunded`.
6. **"Refunded since cancel"** comes from the payment summary only. In the cancel transaction store the summary's `refunded` on the period as `refundedAtCancel: Money`. The limit is `refundableOnCancel − (summary.refunded − refundedAtCancel)`.
7. **A refund on a closed request lowers its total too** (payment, request-neutral). A closed summary (`closedAt` set, ADR-140) holds what the business kept; giving part of it back must not reopen it as a due. `applyRefund` on a summary with `closedAt` also lowers `total` by the refunded amount (never below `allocated − refunded` after the refund), in the same update. Open summaries behave as today (the refunded amount is owed again). Test both, and that a booking refund on an open summary is unchanged.
8. **Books.** The refund payment posts `refund_after_revenue` as today (Dr `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` / Cr cash or `STORE_CREDIT_LIABILITY`). The kept income drops by the same amount:
   - New ORDERED handler `accounting.postEnrollmentRefunds` on `payment.payment.refunded`, `streamPrefixes: ["paymentAccount:"]` (the payment stream; per 3.22 it handles its type and the runner advances for the rest).
   - For each allocation of the refund to a period with `revenueClosedAs` set, one line Dr the income account (`REVENUE_PLAN` for `REVENUE`, `CANCELLATION_FEE_INCOME` for `CANCELLATION_FEE`) and one Cr `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE` for the allocated amount; all lines of one refund in **one journal**.
   - Journal source key: `sourceType PAYMENT`, `sourceId` = the refund payment id, new posting key `enrollment_period_refund_revenue` (no suffix): idempotent per refund.
   - The snapshot comes through the existing posting-source ports: the payment port for the refund's allocations, and a new `EnrollmentPeriodPostingSource.readRefundClose(enrollmentPeriodId)` → `{ revenueClosedAs, currency, locationId } | undefined`. A currency that differs from the refund's fails as `postingSourceMissing` (never posts in another currency).
   - A refund of a non-cancelled period posts nothing new.
9. **Store credit refunds** use the endpoint's `STORE_CREDIT` method, which `creditStoreCreditOnRefund` already handles (entry kind `REFUND`). No new store credit kind.

### Part B
10. **Screens.**
    - The enrollment cancel dialog (3.7 B, `apps/admin/src/enrollment/cancel-dialog.tsx`) shows the refundable amount per period after cancel.
    - A cancelled enrollment's detail page shows a "Refund" button on each period whose remaining limit is above zero (the limit comes from a new field `refundRemaining: Money | null` on each period in the enrollment detail response, computed in Part A by the same function as the reader; add it in Part A).
    - The button opens the existing refund dialog (`apps/admin/src/payments/refund-dialog-lazy.tsx`, 3.30) with the period as the one allocation and the remaining limit as the maximum (add a `maxAmount` prop; the dialog refuses a larger amount before sending). Store credit and the payment methods are offered.
    - An approval-required answer shows the existing approval prompt.
    - i18n BN and EN, mobile layout, initial JS budget 180 KiB for the detail route.
    - Tests: a unit test of the per-period button and maximum; one browser test that cancels a paid GYM enrollment with two paid periods under `UNUSED_PERIODS` and refunds the future period to store credit (the stack runs the worker: wait for results by reloading, never race it).
