## Goal

A staff member can take a storefront product order through courier or own-rider delivery, record the customer's COD payment once, receive money from courier/rider, handle failed attempts and returned goods, and see correct stock, due, commission, reports and balanced accounts.

## Decisions

1. Fulfillment is an Order concern, carrier operations live in courier/delivery, payment owns collection, accounting owns posting. No website balance or stock ledger. Existing FULFILLMENT_METHOD COURIER/OWN_RIDER and shipping statuses become implemented; do not create a competing fulfillment status for the order.
2. Shipping requires a confirmed or packed order, validated destination, an associated manual consignment/assignment, all required advances satisfied, and location permission. Lines and totals freeze at first shipment. First ship commits reserved stock and emits the original order revenue/COGS event once. Failed delivery does not release stock or reverse revenue.
3. Redelivery is the **same goods still in transit**: create a new attempt/consignment reference and set SHIPPED again; never commit stock or recognize revenue again. RTS is terminal. To sell returned goods again, create a new order; never reship a terminal RTS order.
4. Entire-order delivery only in this phase. No partial shipment or partial delivery; RTS accepts a disposition per originally shipped line (RESTOCK or DAMAGED), quantities must sum to all shipped units not already returned. Missing goods are not silently restocked: leave DELIVERY_FAILED until physically reconciled through approved loss/adjustment workflow. Do not fake successful RTS.
5. Collection occurs only on DELIVERED. Amount due is server-read order grandTotal minus net allocated payments; currency must match. Zero-due order records delivery without a zero Payment. Positive amount produces one IN payment COD_COURIER or RIDER_CASH, allocated to the order, in the **same transaction** as DELIVERED. Customer payment status becomes paid even while the courier still owes the business.
6. Courier settlement clears identified collection Payment IDs, not arbitrary order totals. Validate one organization, courierKey, currency and authorized location set; each payment is eligible once. Batch <=100, exact gross=sum selected payment amounts, courierCharges>=0 and <=gross, netReceived=gross-charges. receivedVia is CASH/BANK/BKASH/NAGAD. Underpayment/overpayment is a validation error and stays unsettled for explicit correction; no hidden balancing journal.
7. Rider handover identifies rider and unpaid-over collection IDs, with gross calculated by server. Staff records actual receiving account with permission delivery.cash.handover; the rider cannot approve its own handover. No implication that a POS register received cash unless a real open posSessionId is explicitly validated and existing register port participates transactionally.
8. Financial settlement/handover records are append-only; mistakes create a reversal with reason, manager approval and linked reversal ID. A unique reversal reference stops double reversal. Reversal mirrors journals and reopens eligible collection claims only when the original has no dependent correction; otherwise refuse with explicit conflict. Refunds of delivered orders use existing SalesReturn/payment policy, never a fake RTS.
9. Return-to-sender: restore original shipped units at original cost into chosen state, reverse the original order revenue/tax/delivery-income and stock COGS posting, settle actual advances through existing order termination/store-credit logic. Carrier fees actually incurred remain expenses. Financial entries from a customer payment must not be reversed as if money never arrived. A delivered/collected order cannot use RTS. Delivered-order SalesReturn eligibility uses deliveredAt as its return-window anchor (COMPLETED orders retain completedAt); delivery must not rewrite completedAt to hide the distinction. Refunds after a carrier collection reverse customer receivable/refund liability through existing payment policy while keeping the carrier settlement reconciliation correct.
10. Carrier fees for a failed/RTS shipment are recorded via existing expense API with the consignment reference, not a negative settlement of an uncollected payment. Warn against double recording: a settlement's fee and a separately recorded expense cannot share the same external fee reference. This check is server-side, transactionally claimed.
11. Delivery charges shown to customers are independent of actual courier costs. Add versioned organization-owned DeliveryZone rules in sales: zoneKey, localized name, served administrative area codes, fee Money, active flag. Checkout quotes fee by destination/zone and freezes it on order; caller cannot send amount, currency or arbitrary zone belonging to another organization. Existing geography data may be reused; no new industry-specific address fields.
12. Without a matching active zone, delivery is unavailable; pickup remains offered. Empty or ambiguous overlapping zone rules are rejected at save. Each organization has an explicit server currency; sums use integer minor units. A change after quote forces 4.3 quote refresh.
13. Courier module uses manual courier records with stable courierKey (configured data) and external tracking reference; this is not a provider adapter pretending success. Future 5.2 adapters invoke the same shipping/collection ports via queue. Own-rider assignments validate active membership at order.locationId. No automatic SMS/provider requests in the command transaction.
14. Surface permissions: sales.order.update for fail/reship/RTS; courier.consignment.create for manual courier dispatch; delivery.assignment.assign/complete for rider operations; courier.settlement.record and delivery.cash.handover for funds. Add explicit *.reverse permissions and manager approval for settlement/handover reversals. API guards use keys, not role strings. Customer has none of these permissions.
15. courier/delivery manifests declare actual routes, settings, events, menu and entitlements per catalog; no empty registration. System accounts are seeded once through accounting's existing seed mechanism (COURIER_RECEIVABLE, RIDER_CASH, COURIER_FEES if absent). Do not duplicate an existing account under a new code.
16. All commands require Idempotency-Key, expectedVersion on mutable aggregates, append-only unique financial claim indexes and transaction retries. Audit and outbox commit together. Replay with changed input is refused using existing idempotency semantics.
17. Reports and commission must recognize delivery-channel revenue at shipment per 11, with RTS reversing it. DELIVERED is not another sale. POS sessions are unaffected unless an explicit cash handover targets a register. Backfill old records is unnecessary before the new lifecycle exists; migration seeds new account/permission definitions idempotently.

## Transition and posting matrix

| Command | From → to | Stock | Money/accounting |
|---|---|---|---|
| ship first | CONFIRMED/PACKED → SHIPPED | release reservation + SALE once | order revenue/tax/delivery income; COGS; settle paid advance |
| fail | SHIPPED → DELIVERY_FAILED | none | none |
| reship | DELIVERY_FAILED → SHIPPED | none | none; another delivery attempt |
| delivered | SHIPPED → DELIVERED | none | remaining due → COD collection (or no payment at zero due) |
| receive RTS | DELIVERY_FAILED → RETURNED_TO_SENDER | original-cost RETURN to available/damaged | reverse first shipment postings; actual fee remains expense; paid advance per existing termination rules |
| courier settlement | collections pending → claimed | none | debit receiving account net + COURIER_FEES; credit COURIER_RECEIVABLE gross |
| rider handover | collections pending → claimed | none | debit receiving account; credit RIDER_CASH |
| settlement/handover reversal | original → reversed by new record | none | mirror exact original entries, reopen collection claims atomically |
| return after delivery | existing SalesReturn | existing return rules | existing refund/credit/dues logic, no duplicate revenue reversal |

Collection postings: COD_COURIER debit COURIER_RECEIVABLE / credit ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE; RIDER_CASH debit RIDER_CASH / credit ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE. Existing advance/posting engine determines before/after-revenue state; delivered collections are after revenue. Accounting remains asynchronous, idempotent, ordered through outbox; business records and stock/payment are transactionally synchronous. UI must distinguish recorded event from accounting failure in operations.

## Interfaces & types

Contracts/sales, contracts/courier and contracts/delivery subpaths; Money reuses existing amount/currency shape. Actor is trusted route context, never body.

```ts
interface DeliveryAddress {
  recipientName: string; phone: string; addressLine: string;
  districtCode: string; areaCode: string; postalCode?: string;
}
interface ShipOrderRequest {
  version: number;
  dispatch: { method: "COURIER"; consignmentId: string }
    | { method: "OWN_RIDER"; deliveryAssignmentId: string };
}
interface DeliveryAttemptRequest { version: number; reasonKey: string; note?: string }
interface ReceiveRtsRequest {
  version: number; reasonKey: string; approvalToken: string;
  lines: readonly { orderLineId: string; quantity: number;
    disposition: "RESTOCK" | "DAMAGED" }[];
}
interface RecordDeliveryRequest { version: number }
interface CourierSettlementRequest {
  courierKey: string; paymentIds: readonly string[]; courierCharges: Money;
  receivedVia: "CASH" | "BANK" | "BKASH" | "NAGAD";
  externalReference: string; businessDate: string;
}
interface RiderHandoverRequest {
  riderMemberId: string; paymentIds: readonly string[];
  receivedVia: "CASH" | "BANK" | "BKASH" | "NAGAD";
  posSessionId?: string; businessDate: string;
}
interface CodCollectionPort {
  collect(input: { orderId: string; expectedOrderVersion: number;
    method: "COD_COURIER" | "RIDER_CASH"; consignmentId?: string;
    receivedByMemberId?: string }): Promise<{ paymentId: string | null }>;
}
interface OrderFulfillmentCommands {
  ship(orderId: string, body: ShipOrderRequest): Promise<OrderResponse>;
  fail(orderId: string, body: DeliveryAttemptRequest): Promise<OrderResponse>;
  reship(orderId: string, body: ShipOrderRequest): Promise<OrderResponse>;
  delivered(orderId: string, body: RecordDeliveryRequest): Promise<OrderResponse>;
  receiveRts(orderId: string, body: ReceiveRtsRequest): Promise<OrderResponse>;
}
interface SettlementPostedPayload {
  courierSettlementId: string; reversesCourierSettlementId?: string;
}
```

CodCollectionPort is internal-only; amount comes from current PaymentSummary, no amount argument. HTTP delivery operation identifies order only; client cannot fabricate carrier/rider ownership. Handover receiving member is derived from the authenticated staff actor, never body; it must differ from riderMemberId. All new commands added through owner-module index.ts; routes is the composition root. New event constants sales.order.shipped/delivered/deliveryFailed/returnedToSender reuse declared ones if present; add courier.settlement.recorded/reversed, delivery.cash.handedOver/reversed. Posting key for first shipment remains stable across attempts.

## Data

- Order extends fulfillment with validated address, deliveryZoneId + fee snapshot, shippedAt/deliveredAt nullable, firstShipmentEventId, current attempt ID; existing version/audit. Add {organizationId,locationId,status,createdAt:-1,orderId} for operational queue.
- DeliveryAttempt in sales: deliveryAttemptId, organizationId, orderId, ordinal, method, linked consignmentId/assignmentId, startedAt, failedAt?, reasonKey?, audit. Unique {organizationId,orderId,ordinal}.
- DeliveryZone: deliveryZoneId, organizationId, zoneKey, localized name, areaCodes[], fee, status, version/audit. Unique org/zoneKey; unique active area claim per org (separate claim rows if needed, not an unsafe pre-check).
- Consignment in courier: consignmentId, organizationId, locationId, orderId, attempt number, courierKey, externalReference, version/audit. Unique org/courierKey/externalReference; list org/location/status/date.
- CourierSettlement: courierSettlementId, organizationId, paymentIds, grossAmount, courierCharges, netReceived, currency, receivedVia, externalReference, businessDate, reversesCourierSettlementId?, audit. Append-only, unique org/courierKey/externalReference; unique org/reversesCourierSettlementId partial.
- DeliveryAssignment: deliveryAssignmentId, org/location/order, assignedMemberId, attempt, status, version/audit.
- RiderCashHandover: riderCashHandoverId, organizationId, riderMemberId, paymentIds, amount, receivedVia, businessDate, posSessionId?, reversal link, audit.
- CollectionClaim in respective owner: organizationId, paymentId unique, settlement/handover reference and version. Claim creation/reversal is part of the same transaction as the financial record; ledger originals never deleted.
- All tenant indexes start organizationId; embedded schemas _id:false; monetary sums and eligibility checked inside transaction. List projections exclude full order lines and phone/address unless detail permission requires them.

## Tests (must exist and pass)

- [ ] first shipment consumes reservation and stock exactly once and freezes price/cost
- [ ] failure and redelivery never duplicate stock revenue commission or report totals
- [ ] last-stock concurrent dispatch cannot oversell
- [ ] delivered capture retries create one payment and one collection claim
- [ ] fully prepaid delivery creates no additional payment
- [ ] stale order version or changed due rolls delivery and payment back
- [ ] RTS restores original cost in correct stock state and reverses original journals once
- [ ] RTS refuses DELIVERED order and incomplete disposition quantities
- [ ] paid advance on RTS follows existing credit/refund policy without money loss
- [ ] a delivered order can use normal SalesReturn with correct refund/commission
- [ ] courier settlement rejects foreign courier/org/location/currency/duplicate payments
- [ ] concurrent settlements of the same payment allow one winner
- [ ] gross charges and net balance exactly; no floats or negative receipt
- [ ] settlement/handover reversal is approved append-only and only once
- [ ] rider cannot approve own handover; restricted staff cannot see another location
- [ ] closed POS register cannot receive a rider handover
- [ ] actual failed-delivery fee survives RTS and cannot be booked twice
- [ ] invalid address/zone/fee input and forged price are rejected
- [ ] permission denied and module-off prevent every courier/rider mutation
- [ ] crash after transaction before event handling recovers correct journals
- [ ] shuffled duplicate outbox delivery still gives one ordered posting per source
- [ ] midnight Asia/Dhaka business dates match settlement/report dates
- [ ] day scenario: advance + COD delivery + courier fee + settlement gives zero collection receivable
- [ ] day scenario: failed/redelivered order then delivered return has balanced trial balance
- [ ] day scenario: failed shipment + damaged RTS + fee has correct stock, revenue and expense
- [ ] day scenario: rider delivery + handover + correction matches register and rider cash

## Out of scope

Automated courier booking/webhooks, provider credentials, rider mobile app/maps, COD risk scoring, partial shipments, international taxes, gateway, bulk settlement imports. These remain Phase 5; none is represented by a fake successful adapter.

## Risks & checks

Highest risk: recognizing revenue twice on reship, clearing customer dues before actual delivery, or clearing carrier receivable twice. Transaction/API tests must inject failures after each write. Assert reports after real worker processing, not only mocked ports. Required local touched tests/lint/typecheck/browser checks and full CI; separate T1 review of every financial part. This parent is not merge approval.

## Done when

Staff can operate the complete COD lifecycle and all named end-to-end scenarios reconcile. Delivery methods remain disabled in public checkout until every prerequisite passes. Update 11/14 implemented-phase notes and Phase 5 boundaries, preserving provider automation as future work.
