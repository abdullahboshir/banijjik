# Plan: card 3.2b · Catalog screens

Tier: **T2** · Planned by: Opus 5.5 (Claude) · Implemented by: a Claude Code session · Date: 2026-10-17 · Based on commit: `main 9ceb629`. **Start after 3.2a is merged**; re-read 3.2a's handoff for the final API. Reserved ADR number: **ADR-117**, only if needed.

## Goal
An owner builds the catalog on a phone in their own words: a salon adds "সেবা", a gym "প্যাকেজ", a coaching centre "কোর্স", a grocery "পণ্য". A simple item takes one short form. Options such as size, length or duration expand into variants without a spreadsheet. Images, categories, add-ons and branch prices sit in clear, titled sections (3.13 layout).

## Decisions
1. **Routes and menu** (from 3.2a's manifest):
   - `/[orgSlug]/catalog` (offerings list), `/[orgSlug]/catalog/new?kind=`, `/[orgSlug]/catalog/[offeringId]`
   - `/[orgSlug]/catalog/categories`, `/[orgSlug]/catalog/add-ons`
   - Titles and buttons use the terms: `offering` for the list, `offering.<KIND>` for kind-specific words ("নতুন সেবা", "New plan").

2. **List:**
   - search (name, synonym, SKU, barcode; a barcode scanner typing into the box works)
   - kind chips (only the organization's kinds, labelled by terms), a status filter, a category filter, and `?location=` showing that branch's price and availability
   - cards with image, name, "from" price and variant count on phones; a table from `md`
   - empty state per kind with the add button

3. **Create and edit** (a full page, not a sheet, because the form is long; built from `PageSection`s with help lines):
   1. **Kind:** if the organization has more than one kind, "What are you adding?" with kind cards in term words. Locked after creation.
   2. **Basics:** name, description, categories (multi-select), images (up to 10 with `ImageInput`, purpose `OFFERING_IMAGE`, reorder by drag or up and down buttons; the first image is the cover).
   3. **Price and unit:** price (taka, converted to minor units by a shared money helper, never floats), compare-at price, unit (a select of units with Bangla names), and for weight, volume or length units an optional "sell loose" rule (min and step in the unit's words, for example "২৫০ গ্রাম করে").
   4. **Kind details:** only the fields of the kind (SERVICE duration, buffer and booking mode; PLAN billing period, admission fee, capacity, start rule; PRODUCT shipping and weight), with plain labels and help.
   5. **Options and variants:** "This has options" toggle; up to 3 option names with values (chips). The screen generates the combinations, capped at 100 with a warning, as rows of name, price, SKU, barcode and allowed overrides (for example duration per hair length). Existing variant ids are kept when options change; removed rows are archived as 3.2a does. Without options it is one hidden "Default" variant.
   6. **Add-ons:** pick add-on groups.
   7. **Custom fields:** the 2.5 block for the kind's fields.
   8. **Branches** (only with two or more locations): per location, available on or off and an optional price override, saved through the location endpoints.

   - Save as DRAFT or "Save and publish" (`catalog.offering.publish`). The sticky action bar shows the version conflict handling of the other pages. 409 SKU taken links to the other offering.
   - Archive: confirm sheet.

4. **Categories page:** a tree (indented list, depth ≤ 3) with add, rename, move under a parent, sort up and down, image, and archive (the 422 "in use" message shows the counts).

5. **Add-ons page:** groups with options and price deltas. "Choose one" or "choose several", required, min and max are explained in plain words with an example.

6. **Synonyms:** a small section on the categories page, "Words customers use", with term → synonyms chips. It is behind `catalog.synonym.update`.

7. **Buttons follow 3.2a's permissions:** staff see read-only pages.

8. **Budget and phone:** each route ≤ 170 KiB initial JS (the variant generator and the image list are lazy), no sideways scroll at 375 px, touch targets ≥ 44 px, number inputs with the numeric keyboard.
