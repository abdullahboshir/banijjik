## Goal
Owners and managers open the app and see today at a glance: sales, bookings, enrollments, money in and out, per location or for all their locations, next to yesterday and this month. A reports page shows any date range by day. Everything reads pre-aggregated daily summaries kept by events (AGENTS.md: dashboards never scan), and the home shows only the sections of modules the business has.

## Decisions
1. **Module `commerce/report`** (CORE, `tenantCanDisable: false`: every business has a home). Manifest: permissions `report.home.read` (owner, manager) and `report.daily.read` (owner, manager); menu entry "Reports" (`report.daily.read`); no settings. It depends on nothing at runtime: each source module's facts arrive only while that module is effective, and the home hides sections of modules that are not effective (decision 8).
2. **`DailySummary`** (tenancy LOCATION), one row per `{ organizationId, locationId, businessDate }` (unique index; reads use `{ organizationId, businessDate, locationId }`). Integer counters in minor units of the **organization currency** (field `currency` on the row) plus counts:
   - sales: `ordersCompleted`, `grossSales` (Σ subtotal), `discountTotal`, `taxTotal`, `deliveryCharges`, `netSales` (Σ grand total), `ordersCancelled`, `returnsCount`, `returnsValue`, `returnsReversedValue`
   - bookings: `bookingsCompleted`, `bookingsCompletedValue`, `bookingsCancelled`, `bookingsNoShow`
   - enrollments: `enrollmentsCreated`, `enrollmentsActivated`, `enrollmentsCancelled`, `enrollmentsRenewed`, `checkIns`
   - money: `paidInByMethod`, `refundedByMethod`, `reversedByMethod` (maps `PaymentMethod → minor`), `expensesTotal` (posted − voided)
   - audit fields and `version`.
   Read-time values (never stored): `netSalesAfterReturns = netSales − returnsValue + returnsReversedValue`, `averageOrderValue = floor(netSales / ordersCompleted)` (0 when none), `moneyInNet = Σ paidIn − Σ refunded − Σ reversed`.
3. **Exactly once.** Each handled event increments its summary row with `$inc` (upsert) in one unit of work with a marker `ReportEventApplied { organizationId, eventId }` (unique index, TTL 400 days). The marker is inserted first; a duplicate key means the event was already counted and nothing changes. A redelivery or a replay never counts twice; a failure rolls back both. Handler `report.summarize`, UNORDERED, `eventType` + `alsoHandles` for every event in Inputs.
4. **Which row an event counts in:** the organization-local date of the fact, through the organization timezone (§11 item 13):
   - order completed / cancelled: `completedAt` / the cancel time; location = the order's
   - return created: the return's `businessDate` and location; `sales.return.reversed` (3.5b A2): count `returnsReversedValue` on the reversal's local date. If A2 is not merged when Part A starts, add it when it merges (a one-line `alsoHandles` change with its test).
   - booking completed / cancelled / no-show: the event time; the booking's location; value = the booking total
   - enrollment events and check-ins: the event time; the enrollment's location
   - payments: the payment's own business date and location; method and amount from the payment; a REFUND counts in `refundedByMethod`, a reversal in `reversedByMethod` on the reversal date
   - expenses: the expense's business date and location; a void subtracts on the void date.
   A fact in another currency than the organization's is refused (the handler throws; the event goes to the dead-letter list) so totals never mix currencies.
5. **Source reads** through one port per module, built in `routes/report-sources.ts` from each module's `index.ts` (`readOrderFacts(orderId)`, `readReturnFacts`, `readBookingFacts`, `readEnrollmentFacts`, `readPaymentFacts`, `readExpenseFacts`), each returning only the fields above plus `locationId` and the timestamp. Projections and `lean()`; no list reads in a handler.
6. **Rebuild** (for history before this card and for repair): `npm run report:rebuild -w @banijjik/api -- --organization <id> --from <date> --to <date>` recomputes the rows of that range from the source rows, one keyset batch at a time per source (§11 item 9), inside `runAsSystem(SYSTEM_REASON.REPORT_REBUILD, …)` (new enum value in contracts), replacing the rows of the range in one transaction per day. It never touches markers (live events after the rebuild still count once, because a rebuild of a day that is still open races with live events: refuse ranges that include today and say so).
7. **API:**
   - `GET /orgs/:organizationId/reports/home?location=` (`report.home.read`): today, yesterday and month-to-date (the sum of this month's rows, at most 31 per location) for the caller's allowed locations, or one location when `location` is given (validated against the caller's assignments, ADR-004).
   - `GET /orgs/:organizationId/reports/daily?from=&to=&location=&page=&limit=` (`report.daily.read`): one item per day (summed over the allowed locations or one location), range ≤ 92 days (`VALIDATION_ERROR` above), newest first, `limit ≤ 100`, plus `totals` for the whole range.
   - Request schemas in `packages/validation/src/report`.
8. **Package-aware:** the home response carries only the sections whose source module is effective for the organization (`sales`, `booking`, `enrollment`, `payment`, `expense`), decided by `resolveOrgConfig` effective modules, never by industry (ADR-033). The UI renders what it gets.
9. **Performance:** the home reads ≤ 33 rows per location; the daily read ≤ 92 per location; both with the compound index starting with `organizationId`. No source collection is read on a request.

### Part B
10. **Home** (`apps/admin/app/[orgSlug]/(app)/page.tsx`): inside `DashboardFrame`, cards per section from the home response: Sales (net sales after returns, orders, average order value, vs yesterday), Bookings (completed, no-shows, cancelled), Enrollments (new, activated, cancelled, check-ins), Money (in by method, refunds, expenses, net), each with "this month so far". A location filter (`?location=`) only for members with more than one location. Staff without `report.home.read` see the current greeting only. Empty states for a new business.
11. **Reports page** `/[orgSlug]/reports`: date range (default the last 7 days), location filter, a table of the days (card list on phones) with the totals row, one simple bar chart of net sales after returns per day (hand-made SVG, no chart library: route budget), and CSV download of the shown rows built in the browser.
12. BN and EN keys, money through the shared formatter, mobile first, touch targets ≥ 44 px, budget 180 KiB per route.

## Tests (must exist and pass)
**Part A**
- U1 `the read-time values: net sales after returns, average order value with no orders, net money`
- U2 `the business date of an event is the organization-local date` (a fact at 00:30 Dhaka counts on that Dhaka date; fixed clock)
- A1 `a completed order counts once in its location's day with gross, discount, tax, delivery and net`
- A2 `the same event delivered twice or replayed counts once`; `two parallel deliveries count once`
- A3 `a return lowers net sales after returns; a reversed return brings it back` (the second half when 3.5b A2 is merged)
- A4 `bookings: completed with value, cancelled and no-show`; `enrollments: created, activated, cancelled, renewed and check-ins`
- A5 `payments count in and out by method on their own date; a reversal counts on the reversal date`; `a voided expense subtracts on the void date`
- A6 `a fact in another currency is refused and counts nothing`
- A7 `home: today, yesterday and month to date for the caller's locations; one location with ?location=`; `a location the caller is not assigned to is 403`
- A8 `home leaves out the sections of modules that are not effective`
- A9 `daily: range above 92 days is 400; pages of at most 100; totals for the range`
- A10 `rebuild recomputes a past range to the same numbers the events produced and refuses a range with today`
- Permissions: `a cashier gets 403 on both reads`; cross-org 404.
- Generated module-off and manifest tests pass.

**Part B**: unit tests of the cards (sections shown only when present, comparison with yesterday, empty state) and of the CSV builder; browser tests: after a seeded sale and a payment the home shows them for today (the stack runs the worker: reload until the numbers show), the reports page shows a 7-day range with totals, and a phone width has no sideways scroll; route budgets.

## Out of scope
- The end-of-day SMS or email report (manoxen guide 30), profit and cost reports (the bookkeeping P&L covers them), dues totals on the home (needs its own pre-aggregate), per-cashier and per-staff breakdowns, charts beyond the one bar chart. Follow-up cards if wanted.

## Risks & checks
- Decision 3 is the heart: the marker and the `$inc` commit together or not at all.
- Decision 4: every source must give its local date; a missing timestamp is a bug in the source reader, not a reason to use the UTC date.
- Rebuild vs live events (decision 6): never rebuild today.
