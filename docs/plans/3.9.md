## Goal
A counter sells products and services fast from a registered device: a manager pairs the tablet with a location, a cashier opens the register with a float, every sale is one transaction (completed order, stock, payments, store credit, change), and closing the register prints a numbered Z-report that compares expected and counted cash.

## Decisions

### A1
1. **Module `commerce/pos`** (OPTIONAL, `dependsOn: [SALES, PAYMENT]`, `tenantCanDisable: true`, surface `POS`). Permissions: `pos.device.manage` (owner, manager), `pos.session.open` and `pos.sale.create` (owner, manager, cashier), `pos.register.close` (owner, manager, cashier: the cashier closes their own session; closing another member's session needs `pos.device.manage`), `pos.report.read` (owner, manager). Settings: `pos.requireShift` (BOOLEAN, default true), `pos.allowCreditSale` (BOOLEAN, default false), `pos.receiptFooter` (TEXT ≤ 200, default empty), all with location override.
2. **Device and pairing** (`PosDevice`, tenancy LOCATION): `posDeviceId`, `locationId`, `name`, `status ACTIVE | REVOKED`, `secretHash`, `pairingCodeHash | null`, `pairingExpiresAt | null`, `pairedAt | null`, `lastSeenAt`, audit, `version`.
   - `POST /pos/devices` (`pos.device.manage`, location in the body, validated against the caller's assignments): creates the device and returns a one-time **8-digit pairing code** (from `crypto.randomInt`, stored as an HMAC like ADR-119's OTP, 10 minutes, shown once).
   - `POST /pos/devices/pair` (signed-in member with `pos.session.open` at the device's location; body `{ pairingCode }`, rate limited 10 per member per hour): sets the httpOnly cookie `pos_device` (random 32 bytes, SHA-256 hash stored, SameSite=Lax, Secure outside development, path `/api/v1`, 365 days) and clears the code. A wrong or expired code is one generic `400 errors.pos_pairing_invalid`.
   - `POST /pos/devices/:posDeviceId/revoke` (`pos.device.manage`, `version`): REVOKED; an open session on it can still be closed, no new sale.
   - Middleware `requirePosDevice` (mounted by the POS router only): reads the cookie, finds an ACTIVE device of the path's organization by hash, puts `posDeviceId` and its `locationId` into the request; the member must be assigned at that location. Every POS write uses this location, never a body field. Missing or foreign device → `403 errors.pos_device_required`.
3. **Session** (`PosSession`, tenancy LOCATION): `posSessionId`, `posDeviceId`, `locationId`, `openedByMemberId`, `openingFloat: Money`, `status OPEN | CLOSED`, `openedAt`, `closedAt`, `closedByMemberId`, `countedCash`, `expectedCash`, `variance`, `zReportNumber` (sequence `ZREPORT`, GAPLESS, taken in the close transaction), `zReport` (decision 7), audit, `version`. **One OPEN session per device** (unique partial index `{ organizationId, posDeviceId }` where OPEN): a second open is `409 errors.pos_session_already_open` with its id. `POST /pos/sessions` (`pos.session.open`, `Idempotency-Key`, body `{ openingFloat }`, ≥ 0, organization currency).
4. **POS sale** `POST /pos/sales` (`pos.sale.create`, `Idempotency-Key` required, replay returns the first response). Body: the instant-sale order body of 3.5a (lines, contact, order discount, approval token) without `locationId`, plus `payments: [{ method: CASH | BKASH | NAGAD | CARD | BANK, amount, externalReference? }]` (at most 5), `storeCreditUse?: Money`, `cashTendered?: Money`. **One transaction:**
   - the instant order at the device's location: split `InstantOrderUseCase` into a prepare step and an in-transaction step and export both through sales `index.ts` (behaviour unchanged for `POST /orders/instant`); the POS use case calls them inside its own unit of work;
   - store credit use first (Payment IN `STORE_CREDIT` + `PAYMENT_USE` DEBIT, contact required, never below 0), then each payment as a Payment IN allocated to the order with `posSessionId`, through payment's `index.ts` in the same transaction;
   - **cash and change:** `cashTendered` ≥ the CASH payment amount; change = `cashTendered − cash amount` is returned in the response and printed, never stored as money (§7.1). A non-cash payment above what is due is `422 POS_OVERPAYMENT_NOT_CASH`.
   - **shortfall:** Σ payments + credit < grand total is allowed only when `pos.allowCreditSale` is true **and** a contact is attached; the rest stays a due on the order. Otherwise `422 POS_PAYMENT_SHORT`.
   - `pos.requireShift` true (default): an OPEN session on this device is required (`409 errors.pos_session_required`); false: payments carry `posSessionId: null`.
   - the order's `source` is `POS`.
   If the instant step or the payment step cannot run inside one transaction, **stop and ask**; do not write the order and the payments in two transactions.
5. **Expected cash** (pure domain function, unit-tested): `openingFloat + Σ CASH IN payments with this posSessionId − Σ CASH REFUND payments with this posSessionId`. CARD, BKASH, NAGAD, BANK and STORE_CREDIT never count as drawer cash (they are shown per method). Payments are read through a payment index read aggregated by `posSessionId` (new index `{ organizationId, posSessionId, method, direction }` on payments, read with `lean()` and a projection).
6. **Close** `POST /pos/sessions/:posSessionId/close` (`pos.register.close`, `Idempotency-Key`, body `{ countedCash, version, note? }`): one transaction computes the Z-report (decision 7) from committed payments, takes the GAPLESS `ZREPORT` number, stores `expectedCash`, `countedCash`, `variance = counted − expected`, sets CLOSED, publishes `pos.register.closed` `{ posSessionId }`, audit. A sale that commits after the close read cannot attach to the closed session: the sale's transaction reads the session as OPEN and writes `lastSaleAt` on it, so close and sale conflict on the session document and one retries (the same pattern as 3.11's race marker). Stale `version` → 409.
7. **Z-report content** (stored on the session, `GET /pos/sessions/:posSessionId/z-report`, `pos.report.read` or the session's own cashier): session and device ids, location, opened/closed by and at, `zReportNumber`; sales count, gross, discount, tax, grand total; IN by method; REFUND by method; store credit used; dues created by credit sales; opening float, expected cash, counted cash, variance. Totals from the session's payments and the orders those payments allocate to (orders read by id, one query).
8. **Catalog snapshot** `GET /pos/catalog-snapshot?since=` (`pos.sale.create`, device required): ACTIVE sellable variants at the device's location with `offeringId`, `variantId`, names, price, unit and precision, barcode/SKU, `kind`, `updatedAt`; `since` returns changed rows only; paged with a keyset cursor, ≤ 500 per page (an exception to the 100 limit: compact rows for a local cache; write it in the handoff); index `{ organizationId, status, updatedAt }` on offerings if missing.
9. **Books:** nothing new. Orders and payments post as today (3.24, 3.8). The cash variance is recorded on the Z-report only; posting over/short to the books is a follow-up card.

### A2 decisions (details for 10–13)
- A2-1 **Held carts** (`PosHeldCart`, tenancy LOCATION, `posDeviceId`, `label` ≤ 40, `cart` = the POS sale body without payments and approval tokens, `heldByMemberId`, `expiresAt` = now + 24 h with a TTL index, audit, `version`). `POST /pos/held-carts` (`pos.sale.create`, device required) refuses a 21st cart on the device with `422 POS_HELD_CART_LIMIT`; `GET /pos/held-carts` lists the device's carts (newest first, ≤ 20, no paging needed: the cap is the limit); `DELETE /pos/held-carts/:posHeldCartId` (`version`). Prices are never stored: the sale re-quotes. Another device's cart → 404.
- A2-2 **Price override:** a POS sale line may carry `unitPriceOverride: Money`. It needs the new permission `pos.priceOverride` (owner, manager) **and** an ADR-121 token for `PRICE_OVERRIDE` with `subjectKey` = the instant-order approval subject, `amount` = (list unit price − override) × quantity when lower; an override above the list price needs the permission only. Pricing gets an optional `unitPriceOverride` per line in its quote input (through pricing `index.ts`); discounts and tax are computed on the overridden price; the line keeps `listUnitPrice` and `unitPriceOverride` in its snapshot for the receipt and the Z-report. Without the permission → 403; without the token when one is needed → `403 APPROVAL_REQUIRED`.
- A2-3 **Collect a due at the till:** `POST /pos/collections` (`pos.sale.create`, device required, `Idempotency-Key`), body `{ allocations: [{ requestType, requestId, amount }] (1–10), payments: [{ method, amount, externalReference? }] (≤ 5), cashTendered? }`. One transaction: each payment is a Payment IN with the open `posSessionId` (required when `pos.requireShift`), allocated to the requests through payment `ReceivePaymentUseCase` (request-neutral: whatever request readers accept). Σ payments must equal Σ allocations (`422 POS_COLLECTION_MISMATCH`); change as in decision 4. The requests are read through the readers; a request at another location → 403.
- A2-4 **Cash refunds at the till (returns):** `POST /pos/returns` (`pos.sale.create` and `sales.return.create`, device required, `Idempotency-Key`) takes the 3.5b A1 return body plus `orderId`, and calls sales `CreateSalesReturnUseCase` through sales `index.ts` with a new optional internal input `posSessionId` that reaches the refund payment (`RefundPaymentUseCase` gains `posSessionId` like the IN payments of A1; not reachable from `POST /refunds`). A `CASH` refund then lowers the session's expected cash (decision 5). `pos.requireShift` true → an open session is required.
- A2-5 The Z-report (decision 7) also lists collections (by method) and returns (count, value, refunds by method), read from the session's payments.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-25, lead, after the A1 review `docs/handoffs/3.9-api-review-R1.md`): two more A2 items
A1 is merged. These two items join A2 (same branch, same handoff); they come from review findings F1 and F2.
- **A2-6 Full credit sale at the till.** A1 refuses a priced sale with no tender (`POS_RULE_KEY.TENDER_REQUIRED`) because the Z-report finds a session's sales only through its payments. Fix:
  - store `posSessionId: string | null` on the order (sales record, schema, mapper; set by the POS sale through `PreparedInstantSale`, null for every other order; index `{ organizationId, posSessionId }`, partial on a string value);
  - the Z-report counts the session's sales (count, gross, discount, tax, grand total, dues created) from the orders with that `posSessionId` (one read with projection and `lean()`, through the sales `index.ts`), and keeps the per-method totals from payments;
  - `planTender` then allows zero tender when `pos.allowCreditSale` is true and a contact is attached (the whole total stays a due); without either it is still `POS_PAYMENT_SHORT`. `TENDER_REQUIRED` stays only for the case "credit sale off and nothing paid", or is folded into `POS_PAYMENT_SHORT` (keep the rule key list add-only; an unused key stays defined).
  - Tests: `a full credit sale with no payment is a due when credit sale is on and a contact is attached`; `the Z-report counts a credit sale with no payment`; the A1 tender and Z-report tests updated where their expectation was the refusal.
- **A2-7 Session reads for the screens.** `GET /pos/sessions/current` (`pos.session.open`, device required): the OPEN session of this device or `data: null`. `GET /pos/sessions?locationId=&status=&cursor=&limit=` (`pos.report.read`, location validated against the caller's assignments, keyset paged, `limit ≤ 100`, index `{ organizationId, locationId, openedAt: -1, posSessionId: -1 }` exists). Tests: `the current session of a device, or none`; `the session list is paged and limited to the caller's locations`.
