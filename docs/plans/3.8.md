## Goal
The business records money it receives and returns:
- cash, bKash, Nagad, bank or card
- each payment allocated to bookings and, later, orders and enrollment periods

The module also covers:
- **dues** (what is still owed)
- **store credit**, kept as an append-only ledger
- **manual bKash/Nagad verification** with the transaction id, so one transfer is never recorded twice
- the booking deposit rule that 3.6 left informational

Accounting posts these payments from events in card 3.8c.

## Decisions
**Module** (`commerce/payment`, CORE)

1. **Payment** (location scope, append-only, number from sequence `PAYMENT`): `paymentId`, `paymentNumber`, `direction` (`IN | REFUND | REVERSAL`), `method`, `amount: Money` (> 0), `externalReference?`, `reversesPaymentId?`, `allocations[{ requestType, requestId, enrollmentPeriodId?, amount }]`, `contactId?`, `receivedByMemberId`, `businessDate` (the organization's local date), `approval?`, `note?`, audit fields. There is no `status` edit and no `update` endpoint.
   - **Methods** now: `CASH | BKASH | NAGAD | BANK | CARD | STORE_CREDIT`. The others of `11 §7.1` (`GATEWAY`, `COD_COURIER`, `RIDER_CASH`) are declared in contracts and refused until their cards.
   - `payment.methods` (STRUCTURED setting: allowed methods, plus the business's bKash and Nagad numbers to show customers) decides what the screens offer.
   - **Allocations:** each `requestId` must exist in this organization, and the sum of allocations ≤ amount.
     - A request's allocated total never exceeds its total.
     - An IN payment above its allocations becomes store credit if a contact is given; with method CASH and no contact the difference is change, not stored.
   - **Request types now:** `BOOKING` (and its deposit). `ORDER` and `ENROLLMENT` are accepted in contracts and refused until 3.5 and 3.7 register their request readers.
   - **Manual mobile money:** `externalReference` is required for BKASH and NAGAD, and unique per `(organizationId, method, externalReference)` (unique partial index). A duplicate is `409 errors.payment_reference_taken` with the existing payment's id.
   - **Idempotency:** every create needs `Idempotency-Key` (AGENTS: money endpoints).

2. **Refund** (`payment.payment.refund`): a REFUND payment with allocations to the same requests (not above what was paid to them) and a refund method (cash, mobile money with reference, or STORE_CREDIT). It needs an approval token (`APPROVAL_ACTION.REFUND`, 3.4b) unless the caller is a MANAGER and the setting `payment.refund.managerWithoutApproval` (default true) allows it.

3. **Reversal:** a REVERSAL of a mistaken payment references `reversesPaymentId`, mirrors its allocations, always needs an approval token (`PAYMENT_REVERSAL`), and can reverse a payment only once.

4. **Store credit** (`StoreCreditEntry`, organization scope, append-only, `11 §7.2`).
   - Balance = Σ entries, projected on `Contact.summary.storeCreditBalance` through contact's index, updated in the same transaction with the contact summary's version check so it never goes below 0.
   - Using credit is a payment IN with method `STORE_CREDIT` plus a − entry, in one transaction.
   - Manual adjustment (`POST /store-credit/adjustments`) needs `STORE_CREDIT_ADJUSTMENT` approval.
   - Expiry: `payment.storeCreditExpiryDays` (null = never) and the job `payment.expireStoreCredit`, daily, through `kernel/jobs` from 3.6.

5. **Dues** (`payment.due.read`): `GET /dues?contactId&requestType&page&limit` lists requests with `total − allocated > 0`, from a per-request `PaymentSummary` projection `{ requestType, requestId, total, allocated, refunded }`. It is updated in the payment transaction and fed by request events (booking created and completed set the total).
   - `paymentStatus` (`UNPAID | PARTIAL | PAID | REFUNDED`) is derived from it (`11 §4.3`) and returned on booking reads through booking's port.

6. **Deposit enforcement** (finishing 3.6): with `booking.deposit.required ≠ NONE`, booking confirm (and auto-confirm on create) requires allocated ≥ `depositRequired`, else `422 DEPOSIT_REQUIRED`. Booking asks payment through a port adapted in routes. Cancellation and no-show apply `11 §5.4`: refund or credit inside the free window; otherwise retain the fee, with the event that 3.8c posts as `CANCELLATION_FEE_INCOME`.

**Events**

7. Events (ids and amounts only): `payment.payment.received`, `.refunded`, `.reversed`, `payment.storeCredit.changed`, all with `orderingKey: paymentAccount:<organizationId>`. The accounting stream of one organization stays in order; per-organization order is exactly what 3.8c needs for a correct ledger. Record the throughput trade-off in ADR-123.

**Screens** (`apps/admin`, group `money`)

8. Screens:
   - **Receive payment** sheet, opened from a booking detail and from Payments:
     - amount
     - method (only the allowed ones)
     - the reference field for bKash or Nagad, with the business number shown
     - allocation to the selected requests (the due is prefilled)
     - an overpayment notice ("৳50 will be kept as store credit")
   - **Payments** list (filters: date, method, location) and payment detail.
   - **Refund and reversal** flows with `ApprovalPinDialog` (3.4b).
   - **Dues** list.
   - **Store credit** on the contact detail page (balance, history, adjust with approval).
   - Money input and format helpers from 3.2b; budget ≤ 170 KiB; numeric keypad.

**Records**

   - reference uniqueness

   - store-credit projection with the version check
   - dues projection
   - deposit enforcement
   - the per-organization ordering key
