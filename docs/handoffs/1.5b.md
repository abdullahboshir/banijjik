## Opus review · 2026-10-10 · banijjik-7b
Verdict: **pass**, merged.

Read `handler-runner.ts` and `outbox-dispatcher.ts`.

Checked:
- **Handler and marker:** the handler and its `processed_events` marker share one transaction. The marker is an upsert, so a repeat never aborts the transaction.
- **Context:** a handler runs in the event's organization context, or under `runAsSystem(SCHEDULED_JOB)` for a user-level event.
- **Dispatcher crash:** a crash between enqueue and `markDispatched` re-enqueues after the lock expires. Deduped job ids and the marker make that harmless.
- **Shutdown:** the dispatcher waits for the batch in progress on stop.
