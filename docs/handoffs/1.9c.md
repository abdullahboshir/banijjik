## Explain of the member list query (decision 4)

`find({organizationId, status: "ACTIVE", assignments: {$not: {$elemMatch: {locationId: {$nin: [<A-L1>]}}}}}).sort({displayName: 1, memberId: 1}).limit(21).explain("queryPlanner")`, winning plan:

`SORT (displayName, memberId; limit 21)` over `FETCH (filter: the assignments condition)` over `IXSCAN` **`organizationId_1_status_1_displayName_1`**, bounds `organizationId [x,x]`, `status ["ACTIVE","ACTIVE"]`, `displayName [MinKey, MaxKey]`.

Asserted in `member-reach.persistence.test.ts` (every index name in the winning plan starts with `organizationId_1`). The reach condition is applied at FETCH, so the scan is bounded by the organization's members (limited by the `members` limit); no new index was added.
