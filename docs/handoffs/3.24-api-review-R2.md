## Fix
1. **Stock business date hard-codes the time zone** (`inventory/application/stock-ledger-movement.helpers.ts`, `businessDateOf(date, timeZone = "Asia/Dhaka")`). A business date is the organization's local date (guide §11 item 13). A magic default time zone is wrong for any organization outside Dhaka, and the Dhaka test cannot see it. Fix: give inventory a required time-zone port, the way sales does (`SalesTimeZoneReader`, `buildSalesTimeZoneReader` in `sales/infrastructure/sales-org.adapter.ts`). Read the organization's time zone once in the receipt, adjustment and transfer use cases and pass it to `buildStockMovementDocument`. No default value. Test: one receipt in an organization whose time zone is not Dhaka (for example `Asia/Kolkata` or `UTC`) at an instant where the local date differs, asserting the movement and journal `businessDate`.
2. **The race test accepts a double count** (`accounting/posting-sources-api.test.ts`, "a payment paused after reading CONFIRMED…"). `expect(revenueState === AFTER_REVENUE || settledAdvance === 300).toBe(true)` followed by `expect(settledAdvance).toBe(300)` passes when the payment is `AFTER_REVENUE` and the settle also debits 300 from Customer Advances, which would count the money twice. Fix: assert one consistent outcome. Either the payment stayed an advance (`revenueState` is not `AFTER_REVENUE`) and the settle debits 300, or it is `AFTER_REVENUE` and the settle has no advance line (or no settle entry). Find out which path the code really takes and assert exactly that pair. Also check that `CUSTOMER_ADVANCES` nets to zero for the order after the posting.
