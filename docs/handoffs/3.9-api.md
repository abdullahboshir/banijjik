- **Decisions the plan did not spell out (please confirm in review):**
  - **The order must be visible to the payment step inside the transaction.** `MongoOrderRepository.findById` read without the session, so the payment request reader answered "request not found" for an order written earlier in the same transaction. It now passes `sessionOption(getActiveSession())` (no change outside a transaction). The plan's "stop and ask" point (order and payments cannot share a transaction) did not apply: `MongoUnitOfWork` nests, and the order, stock, store credit and every payment commit together; a failing second payment rolls back the first one, the order and the stock (test below).
  - **`payments[].amount` is what is applied to the bill.** Cash handed over is `cashTendered`; change = tendered minus the CASH part (returned, never stored). Anything above the bill is `422 POS_OVERPAYMENT_NOT_CASH`. `cashTendered` below the CASH part, or without a CASH payment, is `422 POS_CASH_TENDERED_INVALID`.
  - **A priced sale needs some tender** (`422 POS_TENDER_REQUIRED`). Partial payment plus credit sale is the due case; a sale on full credit with no payment at all goes through the order screens. Reason: the Z-report finds a register's sales from the payments that carry its `posSessionId`; a sale without payment would be missing from it.
  - **Z-report access.** Closing and reading use the guard `pos.register.close`; the use case then allows the session's opener, or a member holding `pos.report.read` (reading) or `pos.device.manage` (closing another member's register) at the session's location.
  - **Catalog snapshot** sells `PRODUCT` offerings only (the only kind an order line accepts today, ADR-131) and `limit` counts offerings per page (default 200, maximum 500: the one exception to the 100 limit), so a page can hold more variant rows than `limit`. `serverTime` is the value to send as the next `since`; keep the one of the first page of a full load.
  - **`pos.allowCreditSale`** is `false` in the manifest (plan), but the RETAIL template starts it at `true` (docs/12). The generated module-contract test compared every served setting with `systemDefault`; it now accepts a value whose `source` is `INDUSTRY` (see deviation 4).

## Deviations from plan (and why)

1. **Files outside the plan's table.** `catalog/index.ts` plus `catalog/infrastructure/mongo-pos-snapshot.reader.ts` (decision 8 needs the offering and location rows; no existing export gives them). `sales/infrastructure/mongo-order-totals.reader.ts` (one query for the Z-report), `sales/infrastructure/mongo-order.mapper.ts` (`totalsOf` exported) and the `findById` session change above.
2. **`GET /pos/devices`** (list, `pos.device.manage`) is built: `docs/14` lists it with the other device routes and a manager needs it to see and revoke devices.
3. **Zero-tender priced sales are refused** (see Decisions): a deliberate narrowing of decision 4's "shortfall" so the Z-report stays complete.
4. **Shared files touched, add-only or minimal:** `eslint/restrictions.mjs` (domain may import `@banijjik/contracts/pos`); `routes/tenant-module-routers.ts` (the late routers are returned by a small `laterModuleRouters` helper because `buildTenantRouters` was at the 60-line limit; order unchanged); `modules/core/iam/domain/system-role-grants.ts` points the `pos` row at the new `pos-role-grants.ts` because the table file is at the 300-line cap (OWNER and MANAGER `ALL`, CASHIER `session.open`, `sale.create`, `register.close`, everyone else `NONE`); `test-support/module-contract/assertions.ts` accepts a setting served from the industry template (source `INDUSTRY`) instead of the system default.
5. **The `posDevices` package limit is not enforced.** The plan is silent. The seeded numbers (STORE and CHAT 0, SHOP 1, BUNDLE 2, `ADDON_POS` adds nothing) would block every device of a store that buys the add-on; the owner or lead should decide the numbers and the check (`LimitGuard` in `CreatePosDeviceUseCase`).
6. **`PaymentProps.posSessionId` is optional** so the four other payment creators (refund, reverse, termination settlement) stay untouched and cannot collide with 3.5b; only `ReceivePayment` sets it.

## Not done / known gaps

- Offline selling (owner decision open, default online only). Held carts, price override, collections at the till and cash refunds at the till are A2; screens are B.
- No endpoint lists sessions or reads "the open session of this device". Part B needs `GET /pos/sessions?locationId=&status=` and `GET /pos/sessions/current` (device required); a manager cannot find a session id from the API today.
- Cash over or short is on the Z-report only, not posted to the books (plan decision 9).
- Nothing writes a REFUND payment with a `posSessionId` yet (A2); the Z-report and expected cash already count them.
- The `posDevices` limit (deviation 5).
- `pos.priceOverride` is not declared (A2).
