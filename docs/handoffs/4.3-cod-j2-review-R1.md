## Findings & Verifications

1. **Double-Entry Reconciliation:**
   - COD payment delivery collection records receivable via `METHOD_ACCOUNT_CODE.COD_COURIER -> COURIER_RECEIVABLE` or `METHOD_ACCOUNT_CODE.RIDER_CASH -> RIDER_CASH`.
   - Courier settlement handler posts balanced journal: Dr Cash/Bank/MFS (net received), Dr Courier Fees (charges), Cr Courier Receivable (gross).
   - Rider handover handler posts balanced journal: Dr Cash (till/safe/register), Cr Rider Cash.
   - Both clear collection receivable accounts to 0 balance on full settlement, verified by trial balance queries.

2. **Event Stream Ordering & Prerequisite Verification:**
   - Handlers are ORDERED with prefixes `courierSettlement:` and `riderHandover:`, following ADR-136 convention `/^[a-z][a-zA-Z]*:$/`.
   - As mandated by `4.3-COD.md`, handlers verify that prerequisite collection journals exist before clearing receivable, preventing premature or out-of-order clearance.

3. **Reversals:**
   - Settlement/handover reversals mirror the original posting entry and restore the receivables without data loss.
