|---|---|---|

| **Order placement & guest session** | **PASS** | `CheckoutView` triggers `createGuestSession` ensuring `sf_guest` cookie is established prior to checkout submission, captures name and phone, and upon success clears the cart and redirects to `/checkouts/[checkoutId]`. |
