- **Scope:** Payment COD Collection Adapters & Guarded Correction. Automatic Cash on Delivery (COD) collection and claim-fencing in the `payment` module for orders delivered via `COURIER` or `OWN_RIDER`. When an order is delivered with unpaid dues, an `IN` payment (`COD_COURIER` or `RIDER_CASH`) is recorded atomically with an append-only `CollectionClaim` in `OPEN` state. Payment collections are fenced so generic payment reversal is rejected (409 CONFLICT) while claimed by an active courier settlement or rider cash handover, and settlement reversals atomically reopen eligible collection claims. Reversing an OPEN collection transitions the claim to `REVERSED`.

4. **Payment Application & Use Cases:**
   - `CollectCodUseCase` (`collect-cod.use-case.ts`):
     - Idempotently checks active claim for the order; skips if already collected.
     - Reads payment summary; returns `{ paymentId: null }` if order is prepaid (due $\le 0$).
     - Records `IN` payment (`COD_COURIER` or `RIDER_CASH`), allocates to order, creates `OPEN` `CollectionClaim`, publishes `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` event and audit log.
   - `ClaimCollectionsUseCase` (`claim-collections.use-case.ts`):
     - Validates carrier method and `OPEN` state, marks `CLAIMED` with `claimedByReference`.
   - `ReleaseCollectionsUseCase` (`release-collections.use-case.ts`):
     - Validates carrier method and matching `claimedByReference`, transitions back to `OPEN`, clears reference and increments `generation`.
   - `ReversePaymentUseCase` (`reverse-payment.use-case.ts` & `reverse-payment-write-off.ts`):
     - Enforces claim fence: if claim state is `CLAIMED`, rejects with 409 `AppError` (`payment.collection_claim_active`).
     - If claim state is `OPEN`, reverses payment and transitions claim to `REVERSED`.
