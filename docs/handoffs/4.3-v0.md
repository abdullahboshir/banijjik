## CI fail and fix (2026-11-02)

GitHub run [41325962908](https://github.com/abdullahboshir/banijjik/actions/runs/41325962908) (head `f50669a9`): verify green; storefront browser red.

- Passed: golden path; price-change re-confirm.
- Failed: `recovers when the guest session is lost between quote and submit`.
- Assertion: `expected 'কার্ট (1)…' to contain 'BDT 25.00'`. The cart body was `BDT 30.00`.

Cause: the price-change case PATCHes the offering to `RAISED_PRICE` (3000) and never restores `PRODUCT_PRICE` (2500). The three cases share one `beforeAll` catalog. The session-lost case then quoted the raised total. Guest-session recovery itself was not reached.

Fix in `commerce.browser.test.ts` only: `setCatalogPrice` restores `PRODUCT_PRICE` after the price-change case and again at the start of the session-lost case. Expected totals come from `quotedTotalText(amountMinor)` using `CURRENCY_CODE.BDT` and the minor-unit constants, matching cart `formatPrice`. `BDT 25.00` is the genuine original catalog total, not a leftover after the leaked raise. This closes R1 must-fix 1 and 2 (`docs/handoffs/4.3-v0-review-R1.md`). Nits 2–3 (empty-quote fixture cast, pinning the BN cart title) are left for the lead; they do not affect the CI failure.
