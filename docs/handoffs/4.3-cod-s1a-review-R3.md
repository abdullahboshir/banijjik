- v11 S1a said append the six keys to `SALES_RULE_KEY` **and** `SALES_MESSAGE_KEYS`. `SALES_MESSAGE_KEYS` still has them. Runtime keys are `SALES_DELIVERY_RULE_KEY` on the delivery entry. Handoff deviation 8.
- **Accept.** Putting them on `SALES_RULE_KEY` is what inlined `DELIVERY_ZONE_UNAVAILABLE` into the new-order chunk (measured cause; `order-failure.ts` imports the whole object). `SalesRuleKey` still includes the delivery keys for S1b+ factories. Raising the 180 KiB cap would have been a FAIL.

Admin `@banijjik/validation/sales` imports are type-only on existing order/return/exchange bodies. Stripping the validation barrel re-export is the matching half of the split (zod delivery schemas must not ride into new-order).
